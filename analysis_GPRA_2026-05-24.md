## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### 1a. ROE & ROIC Trends (3-Year)

| Year | ROE | ROIC | Net Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 4.2% | 3.5% | 9.1% |
| 2024 | 5.8% | 4.7% | 11.3% |
| 2025 | 6.1% | 5.0% | 12.4% |
| Q1 2026 | ~3.2% (ann.) | ~2.6% (ann.) | 7.9% |

**Inflection Assessment:** GPRA showed a steady ROE improvement from 2023–2025, suggesting a genuine operating turnaround. However, **Q1 2026 saw a sharp net profit collapse to Rp 6.4B from Rp 37.3B a year prior (−83% YoY)** — a major red flag. The company needs to clarify whether this is a one-time recognition timing issue (common in property) or a structural revenue booking delay.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

- **Tapera & Affordable Housing Policy**: Government continues to promote subsidized housing (FLPP) — GPRA's landed residential segment could benefit from increased demand in the Rp 250M–Rp 500M bracket.
- **Backlog Recognition**: GPRA historically books revenue upon handover; any pick-up in project completions in Q2–Q3 2026 would directly lift earnings.
- **Mixed-Use Developments**: The company's office and commercial leasing segment provides recurring revenue stability during slow residential periods.

### 1c. Free Cash Flow (FCF) Assessment

- **Gross Margin**: 54.8% — exceptional for a mid-cap property developer.
- **EBITDA Margin**: 27.8% — solid.
- **Net Margin**: 7.9% in Q1 2026 (depressed from normative ~12%).
- **FCF Yield**: Estimated ~3.5% at current price. The dividend yield of **5.32%** suggests the company is returning cash to shareholders without heavy debt financing, which is a positive signal for a small developer.

**FCF Verdict:** Positive on gross basis; the Q1 profit slump warrants monitoring of whether operating cash flow follows suit.
