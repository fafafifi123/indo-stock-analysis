## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### 1a. ROE & ROIC Trends (3-Year)

| Year | ROE | ROIC | Revenue (Rp T) | Net Profit (Rp B) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 18.5% | 12.4% | 13.25 | 520 |
| 2023 | 16.2% | 10.8% | 15.32 | 611 |
| 2024 | 17.8% | 11.5% | 17.41 | 700 |
| FY2026E | 18–20% | 12–13% | 19–21 | 800–900 |

**Inflection Assessment:** TBLA shows a compelling **earnings recovery and acceleration** pattern:
- Revenue grew **+13.66% in 2024** to Rp 17.41T — above-trend for the sector.
- Net profit grew **+14.52% in 2024** to Rp 700B — margin expansion is evident.
- The ROE inflection from 16.2% (2023) to 17.8% (2024) signals **operational leverage kicking in** — TBLA's expanded plantation area is now translating into unit cost reductions.

**Key Insight:** TBLA is not just a palm oil play — its **integrated model** (plantation → refining → branded consumer oil + sugar + biodiesel) provides more stable margins than pure upstream producers like AALI or LSIP.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

1. **CPO Price Recovery**: Crude Palm Oil prices are supported by:
   - Strong demand from India (cooking oil imports up 20% YoY)
   - Indonesia's B35/B40 biodiesel mandate driving domestic CPO absorption
   - La Niña weather pattern historically reduces production, supporting prices
2. **Sugar Segment Expansion**: TBLA's sugar business contributes ~25% of revenue — global sugar prices remain elevated, adding margin uplift.
3. **Plantation Area Expansion (+2,500 ha/year target)**: New mature areas entering production in 2026–2027 will increase FFB (Fresh Fruit Bunch) output without proportional cost increase.
4. **RSPO/ISPO Premium**: TBLA's sustainability certification allows it to sell premium to European/North American buyers — a pricing moat.
5. **Biodiesel Contract**: Pertamina supply contracts for FAME (biodiesel) provide predictable offtake revenue at government-set reference prices.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### 2a. The Value Trap Check

| Metric | Value | Assessment |

| Asset Turnover | ~1.05x | Healthy and improving |
| Inventory Days | ~45 days | Normal for CPO; not building up |
| Receivables Days | ~30 days | Tight — no collection risk |

- **Not a Value Trap.** The business is operationally strong — growing revenue, expanding plantations, integrated model with multiple revenue streams.
- **Main Risk**: High debt leverage (~2.0x ND/EBITDA) means the company is sensitive to interest rate hikes and commodity price declines. If CPO drops below $700/ton, FCF could thin considerably.

### 2b. Hidden Assets

| Asset Category | Estimated Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plantation Land Bank | Rp 8–12T | At replacement cost (HGU-certified Sumatera land) |
| CPO Mills (8 units) | Rp 2–3T | Book value understates replacement cost |
| Sugar Mills | Rp 1.5–2T | Operational assets in Lampung and Sumatera |
| Biodiesel Plant (FAME) | Rp 500B–1T | Government-contracted, low-risk offtake |
| **Total Replacement Value** | **~Rp 12–18T** | vs. Market Cap of ~Rp 7.5T |

**Key Insight:** TBLA's **land bank alone** at replacement cost (~Rp 8–12T) substantially exceeds its current market cap of ~Rp 7.5T. This is the core "hidden gem" — you're essentially getting the operating businesses (mills, sugar, biodiesel) for free at current prices.
