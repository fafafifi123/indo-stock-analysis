## 1. The Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend

| Year | Revenue | Net Profit | Net Margin | Est. ROE | Comment |
|------|---------|------------|------------|----------|---------|
| 2023 | IDR 2.8T | IDR 180B | 6.4% | ~14% | Recovery from COVID |
| 2024 | IDR 3.09T | IDR 265B | 8.6% | ~18% | Acceleration begins |
| 2025 | IDR 3.9T | IDR 414B | 10.6% | ~25% | **Major inflection year** |

**Inflection Signal:** This is exactly the inflection pattern GARP investors seek. Net margin has expanded from 6.4% to 10.6% in two years — not from revenue growth alone, but from **operating leverage**: fixed overhead costs spread across higher revenue, resulting in gross profit growing +37.3% faster than revenue (+26.4%). The ROE trajectory (14% → 18% → 25%) is exceptional. The 2025 ROE of ~25% is best-in-class for Indonesian mid-cap construction.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters (Q2–Q3 2026)

1. **Record Contract Backlog Execution:** With IDR 6.87T in new contracts signed in 2025 alone (vs. IDR 3.9T revenue), TOTL enters 2026 with a **backlog exceeding 1.5× annual revenue** — the highest in company history. This locks in revenue visibility for 2026 and into 2027. Construction revenue is recognized on percentage-of-completion method, meaning booked contracts steadily convert to recognized revenue.
2. **IKN Nusantara Projects:** TOTL has positioned itself for IKN Nusantara (new capital city) construction opportunities. The government's acceleration of IKN completion timeline in 2026 creates direct contract pipeline.
3. **Data Center Construction Boom:** Batam and Jakarta data center construction is accelerating (Microsoft, Google, AWS investments in Indonesia). TOTL's expertise in high-spec commercial construction (clean rooms, precision facilities) makes it a prime contractor for this segment.
4. **Affordable Housing Program:** The Prabowo government's 3-million-homes initiative with IDR 240T allocated for 2026 creates a massive construction demand wave. Even capturing 0.5% of this market = IDR 1.2T in contracts for TOTL.
5. **Gross Margin Expansion:** Gross profit grew +37.3% on revenue growth of +26.4% — meaning gross margins are expanding as TOTL moves up the value chain into higher-margin specialty construction.
