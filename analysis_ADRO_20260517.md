**Inflection Signal:** ROE has bottomed near 10–12% — historically ADRO's through-the-cycle floor given ultra-low-cost Kalimantan mines (C1 cash cost ~$35/T). The company remains FCF-positive even in severe coal price downturns. The critical inflection is not coal price recovery but the **SGAR smelter reaching nameplate aluminum capacity in H2 2026**, adding a new, non-correlated revenue stream.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters (Q2–Q3 2026)

1. **SGAR Aluminum Smelter (H2 2026 Catalyst):** PT Smelter Grade Alumina Refinery — a USD 900M+ greenfield investment using West Kalimantan bauxite — is expected to reach nameplate capacity in H2 2026. First full-quarter alumina revenue will serve as a major re-rating catalyst and demonstrate the diversification thesis is real, not just rebranding.
2. **PLTU Batang Power Plant Steady-State Revenue:** The 2GW captive coal power plant provides stable, long-term offtake revenue insensitive to spot coal prices. Power segment EBITDA margins exceed 40%.
3. **Coal Volume Recovery:** Indonesian coal export allocation normalization in 2026 supports volume growth. Domestic PLN demand remains structural regardless of global energy transition narratives.
4. **Special Dividend Optionality:** Management has a history of large special dividends (IDR 175/share in 2024). Given the strong net cash position and post-SGAR capex normalization, another special distribution is plausible in 2026.
