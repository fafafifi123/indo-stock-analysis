## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### 1a. ROE & ROIC Trends (3-Year)

| Year | ROE | ROIC | Net Margin | EBITDA Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 22.5% | 19.2% | 28.4% | 42.5% |
| 2024 | 18.3% | 15.6% | 23.1% | 38.2% |
| 2025 | 16.1% | 13.4% | 20.5% | 36.8% |
| FY2026E | 14.5–17.0% | 12–14% | 19–22% | 35–40% |

**Inflection Assessment:** ADRO's profitability ratios have **declined from their 2022–2023 coal supercycle peaks**, following the moderation in thermal coal prices from >$350/ton (2022) to ~$100–130/ton (2026). This is a **cyclical mean-reversion** — not a structural collapse. The company, now renamed "Alamtri Resources Indonesia," is actively pivoting toward:
- **Green Aluminium** via SGAR (Smelter Grade Alumina Refinery) in Mempawah
- **Hydropower** development in Kalimantan
- **Downstream coal products** (coal gasification, DME)

This transition positions ADRO uniquely as a **"bridge energy" play** — benefiting from thermal coal today while building clean energy assets for tomorrow.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

1. **SGAR Ramp-Up**: The alumina smelter in West Kalimantan (joint venture with Aluminum Corporation of China) is ramping production. This is a high-margin, non-coal revenue stream that the market has not fully priced in.
2. **Coal Price Floor**: Thermal coal at $105–115/ton remains above ADRO's cash cost of ~$45–55/ton. Even at this "moderated" price, the margin remains extraordinary.
3. **Dividend Catalyst**: ADRO's dividend yield of **17.2%** is exceptional — Q2 2026 dividend announcement could be a near-term price catalyst.
4. **China Demand**: China's structural demand for Indonesian low-sulphur coal (ICI-4, 4,200 kcal) continues — ADRO is the dominant supplier of this grade.
5. **Aluminium Super-Cycle Setup**: Global aluminium demand from EV batteries and renewable energy infrastructure is structural — the SGAR investment positions ADRO for 2027–2030 upside.

### 1c. Free Cash Flow Assessment

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Dividend Yield | 17.2% |
| FCF Yield (est.) | ~18–22% |

**FCF Verdict:** ADRO generates extraordinary free cash flow from its coal operations. Even with heavy investment in SGAR and green energy, the balance sheet remains net cash positive. **17.2% dividend yield** is one of the highest in the IDX — this alone provides exceptional income protection.

### 2a. The Value Trap Check

| Metric | Value | Assessment |

| Asset Turnover | ~0.55x | Declining slightly with SGAR capex |
| Cash Cost (Coal) | ~$50/ton | One of the lowest globally |

- **Cyclical, Not Structural**: The revenue decline from peak is coal-price-driven, not operational. ADRO's mine life, cost structure, and infrastructure (Tutupan, Paringin) remain among the best in Southeast Asia.
- **Energy Transition Risk**: Real but 10–15 year horizon. ADRO's pivot to SGAR/hydro is a proactive hedge.
- **China Risk**: ADRO's China customer concentration (~40% of revenue) is a genuine concentration risk — but Indo-China coal trade has structural inertia.
