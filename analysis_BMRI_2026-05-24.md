**Inflection Assessment:** Bank Mandiri's ROE has been on a **sustained upward trajectory**, now solidly above 21% — elite tier for an Indonesian bank of this size. The Q4 2025 net profit of Rp 18.6T represents a **+39.8% QoQ surge**, confirming an acceleration phase. The 2026 guidance of 7.9% loan growth (conservative) combined with NIM of 4.6–4.8% is credible and well within management's proven execution track record.

**Critical Inflection Point:** The Q4 2025 YoY growth of **+35%** is not a base-effect anomaly — it reflects genuine operating leverage as Bank Mandiri's digital transformation (Livin' and Kopra platforms) drives down cost-to-income ratios structurally.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

1. **Livin' & Kopra Platforms**: Livin' (retail) surpassed 30M users; Kopra (wholesale) handles Rp 1.5T+ in daily transactions. Both platforms are generating fee income that is re-rating the bank's earnings quality.
2. **Wholesale Loan Growth**: Infrastructure project financing tied to the IKN (Nusantara Capital) development pipeline continues — Bank Mandiri is the lead arranger for multiple state projects.
3. **CASA Optimization**: CASA at Rp 1,431T (+12.6% YoY) — low-cost deposits form a structural funding advantage. Funding cost advantage vs. private peers is ~80–120bps.
4. **Dividend Payout 65–70%**: Management confirmed a high payout policy, creating a yield-driven floor for institutional investors.
5. **Expansion of Mandiri Sekuritas & Insurance**: Non-bank subsidiaries approaching scale — capital markets fees and insurance premiums adding income diversification.
