## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### 1a. ROE & ROIC Trends (3-Year)

| Year | ROE | ROIC | Net Margin | Revenue Growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 22.5% | 18.4% | 5.8% | +12.3% |
| 2023 | 24.1% | 19.8% | 6.2% | +15.6% |
| 2024 | 25.8% | 21.2% | 6.8% | +18.4% |
| FY2025E | 26–28% | 21–23% | 7–8% | +15–20% |

**Inflection Assessment:** TOTL demonstrates one of the **strongest and most consistent ROE improvement trends** in the Indonesian construction sector. The progression from 22.5% (2022) to 25.8%+ (2024) is driven by:
1. **Premium segment focus**: TOTL specializes in high-specification buildings (hospitals, offices, hotels, data centers) — higher margin work than infrastructure/toll roads.
2. **Operating leverage**: As a mid-cap contractor, TOTL's fixed overhead is largely absorbed, meaning incremental revenue increasingly drops to the bottom line.
3. **Working capital discipline**: Receivables management has improved significantly, with the company maintaining a strong cash position relative to peers.

**Why TOTL is Special Among IDX Contractors:**
- TOTL focuses on **private sector commercial buildings** (not government infrastructure) — this means faster payment cycles, lower political risk, and higher margins vs. state contractors (WIKA, WSKT, ADHI).
- **Track record is pristine**: No major project cost overruns or contract disputes in the past 5 years. Repeat client revenue (hospitals, hotel chains, corporate HQ) is >50%.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

1. **Data Center Construction Boom**: Indonesia is a top-5 global market for hyperscale data center investment (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, GOTO, Alibaba are all building). TOTL's high-spec MEP (mechanical/electrical/plumbing) capability makes it a preferred contractor.
2. **Healthcare Investment Cycle**: Post-COVID, hospital construction and upgrade projects are in a multi-year cycle. TOTL has a strong track record with major hospital groups (Siloam, Mitra Keluarga, RS Hermina).
3. **IKN Nusantara Spillover**: While TOTL is not directly building in IKN, the capital city project creates massive demand for corporate offices, hotels, and residences in Kalimantan — TOTL is well-positioned.
4. **Private Commercial**: Jakarta Grade A office vacancy rates are declining — new office development is picking up. TOTL is the contractor of choice for premium office towers.
5. **Order Book Visibility**: TOTL typically carries 18–24 months of order book visibility — Q2 2026 earnings will reveal new contracts won in 2025H2 that will drive 2026–2027 revenue.
