## 1. The Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### EBITDA & Profitability — 3-Year Trend

| Year | Revenue | Adj. EBITDA | Adj. EBITDA Margin | Comment |
|------|---------|-------------|---------------------|---------|
| 2023 | IDR 12.5T | −IDR 3.2T | −25.6% | Heavy investment phase |
| 2024 | IDR 15.8T | IDR 310B | +2.0% | Breakeven crossing |
| 2025 | IDR 18.3T | IDR 2.0T | **+10.9%** | **Inflection confirmed** |
| 2026E | IDR 22–24T | IDR 3.2–3.4T | **+14%** | Guided by management |

**Inflection Signal:** GOTO has executed one of the most dramatic EBITDA turnarounds in Southeast Asian tech history — from −IDR 3.2T loss in 2023 to +IDR 2T profit in 2025, a IDR 5.2T swing in two years. The Q1 2026 first-ever net profit quarter signals the turnaround has moved from EBITDA-level to bottom-line. This is the **inflection investors have been waiting for since the 2022 IPO**.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters (Q2–Q3 2026)

1. **GoPay Financial Services Scaling:** GoPay became profitable in 2025 with IDR 497B adjusted EBITDA. In 2026, it is ramping its loan book with a target of IDR 8T by year-end. Fintech typically commands 3–5× higher P/S multiples than ride-hailing — GoPay alone could be worth IDR 15–25T at fintech comparables.
2. **On-Demand Services Margin Expansion:** ODS adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 2.1% in 2025 (nearly double vs. 2024). Q2–Q3 are seasonally strong for ride-hailing (summer travel, Ramadan/Eid traffic recovery). Target margin: 3–4% by year-end 2026.
3. **GTV Growth Momentum:** Core GTV grew +49% in 2025 to IDR 400T. Even if growth decelerates to 25% in 2026, GTV hits IDR 500T — providing a large base for take-rate expansion.
4. **Advertising Revenue:** GoTo's ecosystem of Tokopedia, GOJEK, and GoPlay provides first-party data for targeted advertising. This high-margin revenue stream is growing 30%+ YoY and is entirely incremental.

### FCF Yield

- **Status:** Not yet FCF-positive on a trailing basis (Q1 2026 was first net profit quarter)
- **2025 FCF:** GoTo achieved "adjusted FCF positivity" in 2025 — a key milestone
- **2026 Path:** With EBITDA guidance of IDR 3.2–3.4T and capex declining as infrastructure investments normalize, GoTo is on track to deliver IDR 1.5–2.5T in reported FCF in 2026
- **Projected FCF Yield 2026:** IDR 2T / IDR 53T market cap = **~3.8%** — modest but real for the first time
