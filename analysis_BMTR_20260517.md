## Executive Summary

BMTR is the holding company of Indonesia's largest integrated media group, controlling RCTI (Indonesia's #1 FTA television channel), MNCTV, GTV, iNews, MNC Vision (Pay TV), MNC Play (broadband), and RCTI+ (digital platform). The investment case is almost absurdly simple: **total assets of IDR 37.08T** vs. **market cap of ~IDR 2.5T** — a discount of 93% to book value. P/E is just 3.95× on IDR 47.10 EPS (TTM). Total liabilities are only IDR 3.35T, meaning net assets are ~IDR 33.7T against a market cap of IDR 2.5T. The key debate: is this a **value trap** (media structurally dying) or an **asset play** (sum-of-parts far exceeds market cap)? We argue the latter, with important caveats.

## 1. The Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend

| Year | Est. ROE | Est. ROIC | Comment |
|------|----------|-----------|---------|
| 2023 | ~5% | ~4% | Digital disruption headwinds — FTA TV under pressure |
| 2024 | ~6% | ~5% | Digital monetization (RCTI+) starting to scale |
| 2025 | ~7% | ~6% | Stabilization — digital offsets FTA decline |

**Inflection Signal:** ROE is modest but stable — the business is NOT in accelerating decline. The critical observation is that ROE has **stopped falling** despite the secular digital media transition. RCTI+ platform with 50M+ users monetizing through advertising and subscription creates a genuine digital offset to FTA TV advertising erosion.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters (Q2–Q3 2026)

1. **Political Advertising Cycle:** Indonesia's regional election cycle (Pilkada) in 2026 is a significant driver of FTA TV advertising. RCTI as the #1 rated channel captures a disproportionate share of political advertising spend — a cyclical boost to advertising revenue.
2. **RCTI+ Digital Monetization:** RCTI+ platform is scaling toward 60M+ users. Each 10M users added at IDR 30,000/month subscription = IDR 3.6T annual recurring revenue potential — a transformative shift if achieved.
3. **Ramadan Season Content Premium:** Q1–Q2 sees peak viewership and premium CPM (cost per thousand impressions) for RCTI's Ramadan programming. High-rated content drives above-average advertising rates.
4. **MNC Play Broadband Expansion:** Fiber broadband penetration in Indonesia remains low. MNC Play's cable+broadband bundled offering in Tier 1–2 cities addresses a structural growth market.

### FCF Yield

- **Quarterly Revenue:** IDR 2.30T → Annualized ~IDR 9.2T
- **Net Profit Margin:** 5.34% → Annualized net income ~IDR 490B
- **FCF Estimate:** IDR 400–600B (media is low-capex once content library is built)
- **FCF Yield at IDR 2.5T market cap: ~16–24%** — extraordinary; media companies typically trade at 5–10% FCF yield

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### The Core Value Trap Question

| Risk Factor | Assessment |
|-------------|------------|
| FTA TV structural decline | Real risk — linear TV viewership declining globally. RCTI viewership is declining slowly. |
| Digital disruption | YouTube, TikTok, Netflix competing for eyeballs and advertising budgets. |
| Management track record | MNC Group has historically been reluctant to unlock value or return capital. |
| Related-party transactions | Complex conglomerate structure creates governance questions. |
| Minority shareholder protection | Hary Tanoesoedibjo controls >60% → minority interests secondary. |

**Verdict: Managed Value Trap Risk, Not Assured Value Trap.** The key distinction: BMTR's liabilities are only IDR 3.35T — even if the media business becomes worthless over 10 years, the net cash and real estate assets in the balance sheet prevent bankruptcy. The risk is **stagnation**, not destruction. For a 6-month trade (not a 10-year hold), the asset discount provides a trading floor.

## 6. Final Verdict

> ### ⚡ SPECULATIVE BUY (Asset Play)
> **Entry Zone:** IDR 140–160 | **6M Target:** IDR 210 | **Stop Loss:** IDR 118
> **Position Size:** 4% maximum (small position — illiquidity + governance risk)

**Rationale:** BMTR is the most extreme asset-discount situation in this portfolio. IDR 33.7T net assets at IDR 2.5T market cap is not a rounding error — it is a market inefficiency driven by media-sector pessimism, governance concerns, and thin liquidity. The 6-month catalyst is the regional election political advertising cycle boosting RCTI's Q2 revenue. The longer-term thesis is RCTI+ digital monetization. The key downside risk is accelerating FTA TV decline combined with digital monetization failure — a real risk that must be sized accordingly (small position only).
