## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### 1a. ROE & ROIC Trends (3-Year)

| Year | ROE | ROIC | EBITDA Margin | Net Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 8.2% | 6.5% | 24.5% | 6.8% |
| 2023 | 7.5% | 5.9% | 22.1% | 5.9% |
| 2024 | 8.8% | 7.2% | 25.3% | 7.4% |
| FY2025E | 9.5–11% | 7.8–9% | 26–28% | 7–9% |

**Inflection Assessment:** BMTR's ROE declined in 2023 as traditional free-to-air (FTA) TV advertising faced headwinds from digital media migration. However, **2024 shows a clear ROE recovery (+1.3pp)** driven by:
1. RCTI+'s digital platform monetization reaching critical mass.
2. Improved EBITDA margins from content cost rationalization.
3. Pay-TV (MNC Vision/K-Vision) subscriber retention recovery post-COVID restructuring.

The inflection from 7.5% ROE (2023) to 8.8% (2024) and the projected 9.5–11% in 2025E suggests **the worst is behind** for BMTR.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

1. **RCTI+ Digital Monetization**: RCTI+ is Indonesia's largest local streaming platform. With 60M+ downloads, its advertising revenue is growing at 25%+ YoY as brand advertisers shift digital budgets to trusted local content.
2. **Elections & Sports Content**: Major political events and the 2026 World Cup Asian qualifiers drive ad spend spikes. RCTI holds broadcast rights to major sports — ad rates surge during peak viewership.
3. **Pay-TV Subscriber Growth**: K-Vision's satellite TV subscriber base targets lower-income households outside Java — underserved market with structural growth tailwinds.
4. **Hary Tanoesoedibjo Catalyst**: The founder's political connections provide regulatory protection and potential government advertising allocations.
5. **Content Export**: Indonesian content (sinetron, reality TV) has growing demand in Malaysia, Philippines, and Middle East — international licensing revenue is a new stream.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### 2a. The Value Trap Check

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| TV Ratings (RCTI) | #1 rated FTA channel (consistent) | Brand strength intact |
| Digital Audience Growth | RCTI+ MAU growing 30%+ YoY | Successful platform pivot |
| Pay-TV Subscribers | ~3M households | Stable base, room for growth |
| Revenue Diversification | FTA (55%) / Digital (20%) / Pay-TV (15%) / Other (10%) | Good and improving |

- **Is it a Value Trap?** This is the critical question for BMTR. The bear case is:
  - Global shift to streaming (Netflix, Disney+, YouTube) is secular and permanent.
  - FTA TV advertising will structurally decline as audiences fragment.

- **Our Assessment: NOT a trap, but NOT accelerating either.** BMTR has successfully built RCTI+ as a digital hedge. Indonesia's unique market dynamics (large rural population with limited broadband access, strong appetite for local content) gives FTA TV a longer runway than Western markets.

- **The platform pivot is real**: RCTI+ represents the transformation of legacy TV into digital streaming — similar to what SCMA's Vidio did. At current valuations, BMTR gets no credit for this optionality.

### 2b. Hidden Assets — This Is the Core Thesis

| Asset Category | Estimated Standalone Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| RCTI (FTA TV, #1 rated) | Rp 8–12T | Indonesia's highest-rated TV channel — irreplaceable brand |
| MNCTV + GTV + iNews | Rp 4–6T | Portfolio of FTA stations — content/distribution moat |
| RCTI+ (Digital Platform) | Rp 5–10T | 60M+ downloads; comparable to VIDIO at early stage |
| okezone.com + Sindonews | Rp 1–2T | Leading news portals with significant monthly traffic |
| MNC Vision (Pay-TV) | Rp 3–5T | 3M subscribers + satellite infrastructure |
| MNC Play (Broadband) | Rp 2–3T | Fiber broadband network in Java cities |
| Real Estate Assets | Rp 2–3T | Content city complex, tower assets |
| **Sum of Parts (SOTP)** | **Rp 25–41T** | vs. BMTR Market Cap of ~Rp 10.5T |
| **SOTP Holding Discount** | 30–40% | Standard discount for holding companies |
| **Adjusted Fair Value** | **~Rp 15–25T** | Still 43–138% above current market cap |

**KEY INSIGHT — The BMTR Hidden Gem Thesis:**
BMTR is a **pure holding company** that owns some of Indonesia's most valuable media assets — RCTI alone would be worth Rp 8–12T as a standalone listed entity. The market applies a severe holding company discount, but even with a 40% haircut, the fair value is 40–140% above current price. **You are essentially buying RCTI + a free bundle of RCTI+, MNCTV, GTV, okezone, MNC Vision, and broadband assets.**

## 6. Final Verdict

### 🟡 SPECULATIVE BUY

**Rationale:**
- **Extreme SOTP discount**: The sum of BMTR's media assets is worth 2–4x its current market cap even after a 35% holding discount.
- **RCTI remains Indonesia's #1 rated FTA channel** — an irreplaceable asset that would trade at a massive premium as a standalone entity.
- **RCTI+ digital pivot** is real and growing — the market gives this zero credit.
- **14–19% FCF yield** makes the income case compelling while waiting for re-rating.
- **Speculative** tag reflects: (1) R/R of 1.9:1 below our 3:1 ideal, (2) catalyst dependency (value requires an event to unlock), (3) structural media headwinds from streaming, (4) founder-related governance risk.

**Action Plan:**
1. **Accumulate Quietly** at Rp 175–185 — illiquid stock; avoid large single-day orders.
2. **Catalyst Watch**: RCTI+ MAU disclosure, MNC Vision subscriber update, potential subsidiary listing announcement.
3. **Hard Stop**: Rp 150 — below which the market is pricing RCTI at distressed value.
4. **Best Case**: If an RCTI+ IPO is announced at 1/3 of our estimate, BMTR could jump 50–80% in a single session.
