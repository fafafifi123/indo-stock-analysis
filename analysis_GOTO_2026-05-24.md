## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### 1a. ROE & ROIC Trends (3-Year) — Path to Profitability

*Note: For high-growth tech companies, the relevant metrics are GTV growth, adjusted EBITDA trajectory, and path to profitability rather than traditional ROE/ROIC.*

| Period | Adj. EBITDA | Net Income | GTV Growth | Transacting Users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 2025 | −Rp 500B | −Rp 367B | Baseline | 56M |
| Q3 2025 | −Rp 100B | −Rp 120B | +35% YoY | 62M |
| Q1 2026 | **+Rp 171B** | **+Rp 171B** | **+65% YoY** | **69M** |

**THE CRITICAL INFLECTION POINT:** GOTO achieved its **first-ever net profit of Rp 171B in Q1 2026** — a monumental swing from a net loss of Rp 367B in Q1 2025. This is not an accounting trick — it reflects genuine operating leverage as the platform scales.

**Why This Inflection is Sustainable:**
- **69M annual transacting users** (+22% YoY) — user growth is compounding.
- **Core GTV +65% YoY** — order volume acceleration is massive.
- **Fintech Adj. EBITDA +674% YoY** — GoPay and GoPayLater are hitting a profitability inflection of their own.
- **Cost structure is now fixed** — the platform's engineering and operational costs are largely sunk; incremental transactions drop nearly to 100% margin.

### 1b. Revenue Catalysts (Next 2 Quarters)

1. **GoPay Monetization**: GoPayLater (BNPL) now has 20M+ users. At current take rates, this alone could contribute Rp 200–300B in quarterly profit within 12 months.
2. **GoTo Financial Expansion**: Partnership with major banks (including Bank Jago — in which GOTO holds a stake) deepens financial services cross-sell.
3. **Tokopedia Advertising Revenue**: Following the full integration with TikTok Shop (GOTO-TikTok partnership), advertising revenue is a massive untapped stream — e-commerce ad take rates in Indonesia are still below China/US benchmarks.
4. **Gojek Mobility Recovery**: Ride-hailing volumes recovering post-pandemic normalization and urban mobility shift. Higher per-trip revenue from surge pricing optimization.
5. **GoFood GMV**: Food delivery GMV growing as GOTO focuses on tier-2 cities where Grab has less penetration.
6. **AI-Powered Efficiency**: GOTO has deployed AI for driver matching, demand forecasting, and fraud reduction — reducing operational costs by an estimated 8–12%.
