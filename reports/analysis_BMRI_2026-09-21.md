## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN

One week on from Sept 14, BMRI's core fundamental thesis remains intact, but the momentum picture has genuinely softened rather than merely paused. Price eased -1.6% from IDR 4,360 to IDR 4,290 (last confirmed close, Fri Sep 18) across a volatile week that combined two identifiable, non-fundamental drivers: a clean, on-schedule mechanical adjustment of -1.84% on the Sep 16 ex-dividend date, and a -1.38% Sep 18 drop as the now-confirmed Fed hike (25bp to 3.75-4.00%, first in three years) weighed on Indonesian banking names broadly. Both were anticipated in the Sept 14 report and resolved essentially as expected — there is no surprise-shock element here. What is new and worth taking seriously is foreign flow: BMRI recorded a second consecutive net-sell week, and a materially larger one (~-Rp443.2bn vs -Rp251.5bn), making it the exchange's 2nd-most-sold name behind BBCA. This shifts the framing from Sept 14's "ambiguous single-week pause" to a genuine two-week negative trend — still well below the mid-August MSCI-linked peak sell (-Rp815bn in one week) in magnitude, and punctuated by a standalone Sep 17 net-buy day (+Rp208.5bn) on a mining-to-banks rotation that shows underlying demand for the name persists, but a trend that now warrants closer tracking than it did two cycles ago. Mechanically, the pullback again improved valuation and headline reward/risk: P/E and P/B both compressed further below their -2SD bands, trailing yield rose to ~11.1%, and the base-case reward/risk ratio improved to 5.5:1 from 4.0:1 — though this is substantially a fixed-stop artifact (the nominal risk leg shrinks as price approaches the unchanged IDR 4,050 stop) that should not be read as pure thesis validation, especially with foreign selling now trending negative for a second week. Working in the thesis's favor: several domestic brokerages reaffirmed buy ratings this window with targets from Rp5,660 to Rp6,200, widening the observable target range upward and placing our unchanged IDR 5,600 base target comfortably mid-range rather than at the prior week's "upper edge." The rupiah delivered its first genuine step-down this cycle (~17,812 vs ~17,600-655), a modest but real crack in the two-window resilience pattern, while Bank Indonesia's Sep 22-23 RDG — now just 1-2 days after this report — stands as the single nearest live catalyst, with the confirmed Fed hike and weaker IDR both modestly raising the odds of a hold or hawkish lean rather than a cut. MSCI's binding November 2026 decision remains completely unresolved. Net effect: the fundamental and valuation case both remain constructive, and the interim dividend's on-schedule ex-date execution is a genuine (if procedural) confirmation of the capital-return thesis, but a second and larger week of foreign selling plus a weaker currency are real, incremental negatives — the score ticks down again, from 52/70 to 51/70, keeping this a *speculative*, not outright, Buy pending confirmation of whether the foreign-selling trend extends into a third week or reverses.
