| ROIC barely above WACC | Structural concern — limited value creation per IDR invested |

**Assessment: Commodity Play, not Value Trap.** ADRO at IDR 2,280 is cheap on current/near-term earnings but the structural narrative (coal → green transition) limits long-term re-rating potential. This is an **income and cycle play**, not a quality compounder.

### Hidden Assets

- **Adaro Green (Renewable Energy):** Wind, solar, hydroelectric projects in development. Currently unvalued in P/E multiple. If Indonesia's green energy buildout accelerates, this becomes a material re-rating catalyst.
- **BPI (Aluminium Smelter):** Indonesia's first large-scale aluminium smelter. Operational 2026. Long-term contract with automotive/manufacturing clients. Value not fully in P/E.
- **Land Bank & Mining Rights:** Extensive Kalimantan concessions with >30 years of reserves at current production rates
