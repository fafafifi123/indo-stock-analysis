## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

**The Critical Question:** Is BBRI's micro-NPL issue structural (permanent) or cyclical (temporary)?

| Evidence FOR Cyclical (Our View) | Evidence AGAINST |
|----------------------------------|------------------|
| NPL peaked and declining (-177bps) | D/NPL ratio still elevated vs pre-COVID |
| Government KUR program expanding | Micro borrower real income still stressed |
| Agricultural economy recovering | IDR at 17,780 hurts import costs for micro |
| Management provisioned aggressively in 2024 | BI rate 5.50% increases repayment burden |

**Verdict: CYCLICAL, not structural.** The micro-NPL cycle peaked definitively in Q1 2025 and is now on a downward trajectory. The BI rate hike (to defend IDR and reduce import inflation) is actually neutral-to-positive for BBRI long-term, as:
1. IDR stabilization reduces cost-push inflation for micro borrowers
2. Higher lending rates improve NIM (micro rates reprice quickly)
3. 13.3% dividend yield provides exceptional downside protection

### Hidden Assets

- **BRI Agro (BRI Finance):** Subsidiary focused on agricultural financing; undervalued on BBRI's books
- **BRILink Agent Network:** 700,000+ BRILink agents = financial inclusion infrastructure with embedded network value
- **BRI Ventures:** Startup ecosystem plays (fintech, agritech) not reflected in banking P/E
- **Implicit Government Guarantee:** As Indonesia's largest bank by branches and accounts, systemic importance = embedded put option vs. existential risk
