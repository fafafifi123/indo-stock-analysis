### Biodiesel Catalyst — The Bull Case

This is the ONE compelling reason to look at TBLA:

| Period | Biodiesel Revenue | YoY Change |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| H1 2024 | IDR 1.5T | Base |
| H1 2025 | IDR 4.1T | **+173%** |

Indonesia's policy progression:
- B30 (2020) → B35 (2023) → **B40 (Jan 2025)** → **B50 (2026 planned)**

TBLA's biodiesel capacity expanded:
- Plant 1: ~1,050 TPD
- Plant 2 (completed 2024): +450,000 TPY additional
- **Total: 765,000 TPY** — positioned for B50 ramp

FY2025 biodiesel contract: 809,000 KL (2x FY2024). This is government-mandated demand — not subject to market competition.

**If biodiesel revenue doubles in FY2025 (as H1 data suggests), TBLA's FY2025 net income could reach IDR 1.0-1.2T (+43-71% YoY)**, which would re-rate the stock significantly.

### Economic Moat

**Moat Type: Regulated-Market Moat (Conditional)**

TBLA's biodiesel moat is essentially a **regulatory moat** — the government forces Pertamina to buy biodiesel from certified producers like TBLA at mandated prices. This is not a free-market moat; it depends entirely on:
1. Government maintaining B40/B50 mandate
2. FAME (biodiesel) contracts being renewed annually
3. CPO prices staying within profitable range

If the biodiesel mandate is reversed or delayed (Indonesia delayed B40 once before), TBLA's revenue drops sharply.

**Sugar segment:** Stable but slow-growth. Price regulated domestically. Not a growth driver.

## 2. Governance & Risk

### Corporate Governance (GCG)

**GOVERNANCE IS TBLA'S BIGGEST STRUCTURAL PROBLEM**

- **Score: 8/54 = 14.8% — Near Bottom of Global Rankings**
- Widarto Oey (Executive Chairman) and Santoso Winata (President Commissioner) are brothers — the same family controls both executive and supervisory roles simultaneously. This fundamentally undermines independent oversight.
- Subsidiary PT DGS: TBLA claims de facto control through its controlling shareholders' direct ownership of DGS — effectively a shadow consolidation without formal consolidation
- No independent oversight of related-party transactions of significance
- Moody's withdrew its rating, suggesting investor communication is inadequate

### Value Trap Assessment

**HIGH VALUE TRAP RISK**

| Factor | Signal |

| Structural decline? | No — biodiesel growing |
| Poor governance? | YES — confirmed (8/54 score) |
| Earnings not converting to cash? | YES — negative op. CF |
| Leverage rising? | YES — D/E 193% and growing |
| Commodity cycle dependence? | YES — CPO price risk |
| Management alignment? | QUESTIONABLE — family control |

**TBLA exhibits 4 of 6 classic value trap indicators.** The biodiesel catalyst is genuine, but governance failure and balance sheet risk make this a risky bet. The stock could remain cheap for years while management extraction erodes shareholder value.
