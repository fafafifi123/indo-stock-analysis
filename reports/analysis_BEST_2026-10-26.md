## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

**Value trap check:** industrial estates are cyclical, but Bekasi/Cikarang land is scarce and demand from data centers and manufacturers is structural, not declining. The risk is lumpiness: H2 land sales may not repeat H1. **Hidden assets:** land bank (inventory) is held at historical cost; its market value per hectare is probably above book (unquantified, no land-bank data found). Asset turnover is low by nature; inventory is the asset, not a warning sign.
