## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

GOTO still does **not** screen as a classic value trap — the fundamental case is, if anything, slightly stronger than Aug 12 (fintech guidance raised, group EBITDA guidance held despite a real regulatory headwind). But the **mechanical/technical risk has genuinely intensified**, not eased, since Aug 12: (a) the MSCI binary event resolved to the bearish side, confirming real (if modest, ~IDR 1T) forced outflows; (b) the sell queue at the floor has more than doubled to ~246M lots, a worse overhang than the "healthy two-way participation" picture described on Aug 12; (c) the IDX is now actively engineering the removal of the very price mechanism that has kept the stock nominally at Rp50 — a structural change, not organic price discovery, and one whose first-order effect on a stock with a 246M-lot sell overhang is more likely to be downward than upward in the days immediately after implementation. The **cash-per-share floor of ~IDR 17.3** is the hardest evidence that the business itself is not the source of risk here — even a disorderly mechanical repricing would need to fall roughly 65% from Rp50 before threatening the net-cash margin of safety, which is a very wide berth. This remains a technical trap layered on an improving business, but the technical trap has gotten **materially worse**, not better, over the past two weeks.

### Stress Tests

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500
Largely unchanged from Aug 12: GOTO's revenue base is overwhelmingly domestic-GTV-denominated with limited direct FX exposure. A ~10% depreciation from the current ~17,665 would erode the real USD value of the IDR 23.7T cash pile and would likely coincide with an EM risk-off move that halts the IHSG's rally (now +2.4% further extended since Aug 12), which would also complicate the timing of the IDX's planned floor-removal rollout. Moderate negative, not thesis-breaking.

#### BI Rate +50bps
A hike to 6.25% has negligible direct effect on GOTO given its near-zero interest-bearing debt, but the DCF discount rate would tick up ~50–100bps, compressing base-case intrinsic value by roughly IDR 4–6/share. The new wrinkle since Aug 12: this decision would now be taken under Acting Governor Destry Damayanti, whose policy framework is less battle-tested than Perry Warjiyo's — a modest incremental uncertainty for the whole IDX, GOTO included, though the Aug 18–19 hold (her first meeting) was read as reassuringly conventional.

#### MSCI Exclusion — Effective Date Execution Risk (Aug 31, 2026)
The binary decision itself is now resolved (excluded), so the remaining risk is execution around the **Aug 31, 2026** effective date: whether realized passive outflows land near Mirae Asset's ~IDR 1T estimate or run larger if additional funds beyond the pure MSCI trackers de-risk ahead of the rebalancing. Because the stock has been functionally untradeable at Rp50 since May, much of this flow may already be reflected in the swollen sell queue rather than being a fresh shock on Aug 31 itself — but this is an assumption, not a confirmed fact, and the effective date itself (one week before the IDX's own floor-removal test window) is worth watching closely as a potential compounding event.

#### NEW — IDX Rp50 Floor Removal (Targeted September 7, 2026)
This is the dominant new risk/catalyst not present in the Aug 12 report. BEI plans to lower the minimum tradeable price from Rp50 to Rp1 across the regular and cash markets, with member testing on Aug 22 and 29 and implementation targeted for **September 7, 2026**. With a reported ~246 million lots queued for sale at Rp50, removing the mechanical floor into that overhang is far more likely to produce an initial **gap down** than an orderly re-rating — this is the basis for this report's much wider Bear case (IDR 35 vs. IDR 42 on Aug 12) and lower hard stop (IDR 38 vs. IDR 45). The offsetting consideration: once the sell overhang genuinely clears at a market-clearing price, real price discovery can finally begin for the first time since May — which is arguably a **necessary precondition**, not an obstacle, for GOTO's improving fundamentals to eventually be reflected in its share price. This is a two-sided event that should be watched in real time as the September 7 date approaches, and the next report in this series should treat it as the single most important open question.

## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — DOWNGRADED from 💎 Strong Buy (Gem)

GoTo's business continues to execute roughly as described on Aug 12 — the fintech segment's guidance was raised mid-cycle even as group EBITDA guidance held firm through a real regulatory headwind, and nothing in the past two weeks suggests the underlying turnaround has stalled. But the technical and structural picture, which was already the stated risk in the Aug 12 report, has moved decisively in the wrong direction rather than resolving favorably: the same-day MSCI decision that was pending on Aug 12 came back **against** GoTo (confirmed exclusion from the Global Standard Index, effective Aug 31), the sell queue at the Rp50 floor has more than doubled to ~246 million lots, and — critically — the IDX has now scheduled the **removal of the Rp50 mechanical floor itself** for as early as September 7, 2026, a genuinely new structural risk this framework had not contemplated. Reward-to-risk has compressed from 5.6:1 to 2.2:1 as a direct consequence, and the score has fallen from 54 to 48 — below the level this framework has historically treated as top-conviction Gem territory. This is a downgrade to Speculative Buy, not an exit: the ~IDR 17.3/share cash-per-share anchor, the ~35%-of-market-cap net-cash cushion, and the still-intact multi-quarter profitability trend mean the fundamental thesis is unbroken, and a disorderly repricing around the floor-removal event could well be the catalyst that finally lets genuine price discovery — and eventually a re-rating toward the DCF Base case of ~IDR 80 — begin. But position sizing should now explicitly account for the real possibility of a sharp, mechanically-driven move to the mid-30s/low-40s in the two-to-four-week window around September 7, before any recovery plays out. Existing holders should tighten risk management around the new IDR 38 reference stop; new capital should size for near-term volatility that is meaningfully higher than the Aug 12 report anticipated, even though the underlying GARP case remains intact.
