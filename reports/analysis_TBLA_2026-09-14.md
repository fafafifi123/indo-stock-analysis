## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN, Conviction Trimmed Modestly

This is the first genuinely two-sided window in this series. TBLA's price fell a further ~2.3% (640→625, confirmed Sept 11 close), extending its cumulative decline since Aug 12 to roughly −12.6% — but for the first time, this occurred alongside a broad-market pullback (IHSG: fresh high 6,686 on Sept 8, back to 6,541 by Sept 11) rather than against a rallying index, weakening the clean "idiosyncratic mispricing" narrative used in the Aug 24 and Sept 7 reports without erasing the wide cumulative gap (TBLA −12.6% vs. IHSG +2.5% since Aug 12). The central B50 catalyst kept advancing — nationwide station coverage now reported at ~94%, with the residual gap reduced to a small, dated transition tail running to September 30 — but CPO, previously an unambiguous tailwind, posted its first confirmed pullback of the series (RM4,950–5,018 → RM4,814, with forward guidance stepped down to RM4,400–4,600). Bank Indonesia has still not met since Aug 18–19; the **Sept 22–23 RDG is now roughly a week away** and remains the single largest open risk to the leverage-sensitive balance sheet (D/E 193%, interest coverage ~1.9x, both unverified pending the Oct 30 Q3 filing). We maintain Speculative Buy, hold both the base target (IDR 1,100) and hard stop (IDR 580) flat, and note the R/R improvement to 10.56:1 is mechanical — a function of the lower entry price, not an improved fundamental picture. The stop buffer has compressed to its tightest point yet (−7.2%), and a breach should now be read as a genuine stress signal rather than routine noise. Score moves down modestly, from 52 to 50, reflecting a week where the growth catalyst firmed but the macro/commodity backdrop turned mixed for the first time — this remains a high-conviction, high-volatility satellite position, but the easy, one-directional read of prior reports is no longer available.
