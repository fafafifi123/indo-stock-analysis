### Stress Tests

#### MSCI Decision — RESOLVED
GOTO removed from MSCI Global Standard Index, effective after close Aug 31, 2026, on stated grounds of index-replicability/liquidity issues — not a performance judgment. Aletheia Capital's Angus Mackintosh has now confirmed on the record the "minimal impact" framing cited in the baseline's stress test. Net effect: the single largest identified near-term binary risk is now resolved, removing an overhang, even though the outcome itself (exclusion rather than retention) is the less favorable of the two possibilities.

#### NEW / sharpened — Full Call Auction (FCA) proximity
Reporting this window clarified a concrete mechanical trigger previously underweighted: if GOTO's average regular-market price stays below ~Rp51 for approximately six months, it risks placement on the IDX Special Monitoring Board under Full Call Auction rules — at which point the Rp50 floor protection disappears and the share price could theoretically fall toward Rp1. Measured from the ~May 5–13, 2026 pinning date, this ~6-month threshold falls around **November 2026** — within the 6-month target horizon. This is the mechanical analog of the existing Hard Stop (45, triggers on FCA/floor removal), now closer in time than at Aug 12, and is the primary reason the Bear case and discount widened this window, not new fundamental deterioration.

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500 — Moderate Negative (unchanged)
Erodes the real USD value of the cash pile, could stall the IHSG rally that supports eventual float normalization.

#### BI Rate +50bps — Near-negligible direct, modest indirect (unchanged)
Minimal direct effect given near-zero debt, but ticks the DCF discount rate up ~50–100bps, compressing intrinsic value ~IDR 4–6/share. The Aug 19–20 BI meeting falls just after this report.
