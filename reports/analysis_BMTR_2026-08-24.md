## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check
**Case for trap (unchanged):** Revenue is shrinking (-4.6% YoY, FY2025) as legacy free-to-air TV advertising secularly declines; ROE (~4%) is mediocre in absolute terms; free float is thin (majority ownership sits with Hary Tanoesoedibjo/MNC Group entities, with Lo Kheng Hong holding a further ~6.4% / ~1.06B shares), which structurally caps re-rating velocity; conglomerate/holdco discounts on Indonesian media groups have persisted for years without closing.

**Case against trap (strengthened):** Net income still grew +21.9% YoY on margin discipline alone; the balance sheet is genuinely conservative (0.24x D/E, 3.74x current ratio); an independent, AAA-rated guarantor (CGIF/ADB) was willing to fully backstop IDR 800B of new paper in June 2026 and that guarantee rating has been reaffirmed since with no adverse action; and now a second consecutive BI rate hold (Aug 18–19) removes a further source of margin pressure on the newly-fixed debt stack. A stock can be statistically cheap and still be a trap; a stock that a AAA guarantor underwrote at 7% and that has traded through 49 event-free days is progressively weaker evidence for "trap."

**Verdict:** Unchanged — not a classic value trap, more accurately a persistent, structural conglomerate discount on real, cash-generative media/content assets, with a near-term solvency question that keeps getting more (not less) resolved by the passage of time without incident.
