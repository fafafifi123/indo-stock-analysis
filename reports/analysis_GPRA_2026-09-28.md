## Final Verdict: 🔴 AVOID (Maintained)

Nothing that happened between September 14 and September 28, 2026 resolves the core thesis, and on balance the picture is modestly worse. This cycle's research also surfaced newly-found Q1 and Q2 2026 interim financials showing a steeper YoY earnings decline (Q1 net income -82.9% YoY) than the already-known FY2025 trend, only partially offset by a real Q2 sequential rebound. Combined with a confirmed downtrend on elevated (distribution-looking) volume, a sharper broad-market risk-off, a weaker Rupiah, and a more decisively hawkish Fed/BI backdrop, the score drifts down one net point to 24/70. The upcoming Q3 2026 results release (October 30) and the next BI RDG (likely mid-to-late October) remain the two scheduled events most likely to move this thesis in either direction.
