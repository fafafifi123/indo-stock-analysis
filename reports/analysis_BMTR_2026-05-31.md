## 1. The "Gem" Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend with Inflection Analysis

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | Trend |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Revenue (IDR T) | ~10.5T | ~10.8T | ~10.06T | **9.59T** | ⚠️ Declining (-4.6%) |
| Net Income (IDR B) | ~820B | ~700B | ~538B | **1,110B** | 🟢 Sharp reversal! |
| EBITDA (IDR T) | ~3.1T | ~2.9T | ~2.8T | **3.04T** | ⚠️ Flat with uptick |
| EBITDA Margin | 29.5% | 26.9% | 27.9% | **29.5%** | 🟢 Margin recovery |
| D/E Ratio | 0.35x | 0.30x | 0.27x | **0.24x** | 🟢 Leverage declining |

**INFLECTION POINT — FY2025:** The most surprising data point: **net income surged 106% to IDR 1.11T** in FY2025, even as revenue declined 4.62%. This margin expansion suggests aggressive cost-cutting, subsidiary restructuring, or one-time gains. At EBITDA margin of 29.5%, the core media operations remain highly cash-generative.

**Warning:** Q1 2026 net income was IDR 51.1B (vs Q4 2025 IDR 134.1B, -61.9% QoQ) — suggesting the FY2025 net income spike may have included non-recurring items. This is a key risk to verify.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **FTA TV remains dominant in Indonesia:** 76%+ of Indonesians watch FTA TV daily. RCTI, MNCTV, GTV, and iNews collectively command the largest combined audience share in Indonesia.
2. **Digital advertising transition:** BMTR is pivoting advertising inventory to digital/streaming (MNC Vision, Mister Aladin, Okezone.com). Online portals showing growth amid FTA TV ad revenue plateau.
3. **Content monetization:** MNC's content library is one of Indonesia's largest. Licensing revenue from regional OTT platforms (Netflix, Disney+) represents an undermonetized asset.
4. **Bond issuance → growth capex:** IDR 800B bonds issued in 2026 (7.03% coupon) suggest management is investing, not contracting.

### Cash Strength & FCF

- **EBITDA:** IDR 3.04T (FY2025) — strong cash generation from media operations
- **FCF:** EBITDA of IDR 3T minus capex (~IDR 800B) minus interest (~IDR 500B) = ~IDR 1.7T FCF
- **FCF Yield:** IDR 1.7T / IDR 7.8T market cap = **21.8%** — extraordinarily high
- **D/E:** 0.24x — very low leverage; total debt ~IDR 6.8T vs equity IDR 28.21T
- No dividend paid — all FCF retained or used for subsidiary investment

**Key concern:** Revenue is declining. The 21.8% FCF yield is compelling, but only if revenue stabilizes. If TV advertising continues declining, EBITDA will follow.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### The "Value Trap" Check

| Diagnostic | Assessment | Signal |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Revenue trend | -4.62% FY2025, -1% FY2024 | ⚠️ Structural or cyclical? |
| EBITDA margin | 29.5% (stable/improving) | ✅ Cost structure healthy |
| Audience share | RCTI #1 in FTA TV Indonesia | ✅ Market leadership intact |
| Digital transition progress | MNC Vision, OTT, portals | ⚠️ Unclear pace |
| Advertising market share | Losing ground to Google/Meta | 🔴 Secular headwind |
| D/E ratio trend | 0.35x → 0.24x (improving) | ✅ Balance sheet getting cleaner |

**Value Trap Risk Assessment:** MEDIUM-HIGH

**Bull Case (Not a trap):** Revenue decline is mild (-1 to -5%), EBITDA margins are stable/improving, and management is pivoting to digital/streaming. At 4.6x EV/EBITDA, the stock is priced as if the business is dying — but it isn't dying, just transforming. The FCF yield of 21.8% means the company can reinvest into its own shares or grow into new verticals.

**Bear Case (Could be a trap):** Digital advertising (Google, Meta, TikTok) is permanently taking TV ad dollars. Without a breakthrough in streaming or digital advertising, BMTR may see EBITDA erode 3-5% annually for years, making the current cheap valuation a mirage.

**Verdict:** Not a pure value trap — the asset base is real and valuable — but the path to re-rating requires an identifiable digital catalyst.
