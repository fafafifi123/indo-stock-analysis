### Stress Test 1: IDR Weakens vs USD (IDR → 18,000)

- TOTL revenues are IDR-denominated (domestic construction)
- Some materials (steel, imported equipment) priced in USD → cost inflation
- Impact: gross margin compression ~2-3% → net income ~-10%
- Price impact: IDR 1,075 → ~IDR 950 (-12%)
- **Key mitigant:** TOTL's data center contracts are with multinational companies (Google, Microsoft) who can absorb or renegotiate for FX movements
- **Verdict:** Manageable. Even in this scenario, P/E at IDR 950 is only 8.9x — still cheap

### Stress Test 2: BI Raises Rates by 50bps

- TOTL is DEBT-FREE — no direct interest rate impact whatsoever
- Indirect: higher rates could slow private commercial construction (office buildings)
- Data center investment is NOT rate-sensitive (hyperscaler capex is budget-driven, not financed)
- Healthcare construction is government-mandated
- Net income impact: ~-3-5% (only via indirect demand effect)
- **Verdict: MOST RATE-INSENSITIVE STOCK IN THIS UNIVERSE — zero debt = zero rate sensitivity**
