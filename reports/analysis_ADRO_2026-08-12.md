## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

**Verdict: cyclical, not structural — but the discount has narrowed.** The 2025 demerger materially de-risked the "energy transition" question: the thermal-coal export business (the segment most exposed to power-sector decarbonization) now sits in separately-listed AADI, while Alamtri (ADRO) retained (1) metallurgical coal — linked to steel/EV supply chains rather than thermal power, (2) asset-light mining services (SIS), and (3) the KAI aluminum smelter/Mentarang hydropower platform, which is itself an energy-transition-aligned growth asset (aluminum demand tied to EV/renewables buildout). This is a controlled corporate rotation away from thermal coal risk, not a melting ice cube.

What has changed since July 4 is valuation, not thesis: forward P/E has re-rated from ~4.5x to ~6.7x and the stock trades within 7% of its 52-week high, so the "deep value" gap that justified aggressive accumulation in early 2026 has partially closed. Remaining risks are execution (smelter ramp timing), cyclicality (metcoal ASP still coal-price-linked), and a macro overhang (MSCI) unrelated to fundamentals.

### Stress Tests

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500 — POSITIVE
ADRO/Alamtri's metcoal and mining-services revenue is USD-linked while a meaningful share of operating costs are IDR-denominated; a weaker rupiah (from ~17,850 to 19,500, roughly -9%) would expand IDR-reported margins and dividend capacity, replicating the hedge dynamic that drove ADRO's outperformance vs. IHSG earlier in 2026.

#### BI Rate +50bps — Neutral
D/E of just 0.12x means ADRO carries minimal rate-sensitive IDR debt, and its revenue base is largely USD. A further BI hike would primarily pressure domestic-demand sectors (banks, property, consumer) and could compress the broader IHSG multiple, but the direct P&L impact on ADRO is limited.

#### MSCI Frontier Downgrade — November 2026 — MODERATE NEGATIVE
Indonesia retained EM status in the June 2026 review but the outlook was cut to negative, with the Foreign Inclusion Factor freeze extended and November flagged as the critical checkpoint; market-wide passive-outflow risk is estimated at $2.2–13B. As a large, liquid, dividend-paying constituent, ADRO would likely see proportional index-fund selling in a downgrade scenario — but domestic institutional demand, family/strategic ownership, and a high dividend yield provide a partial floor, making the impact moderate rather than severe. This is the key swing risk to monitor into year-end.

## Final Verdict: 🟡 Hold (Income) — MAINTAIN

ADRO/Alamtri's underlying operating story continues to improve — 1Q26 net income +67% YoY, metcoal volumes tracking +11.5% guidance, ROIC nearly doubling off its FY2025 demerger-driven trough, and the KAI aluminum smelter on track to reach full production and begin meaningful revenue contribution by Q4 2026 — genuine progress on the green-energy/metals diversification thesis flagged in the July report. However, FY2026 net income guidance has been quietly trimmed to ~$605M (+35.2%) from the $719M (+60%) pace cited on July 4, reflecting only a partial ASP recovery ($130/t vs. the sharper rebound the market had hoped for. Combined with the stock's 9.6% rally since the last report — pushing forward P/E from ~4.5x to ~6.7x and price to within 7% of its 52-week high — the risk/reward has compressed further, from 1.56:1 to 1.43:1. This is not a sell signal: the dividend yield (10.9%), balance sheet (D/E 0.12x), and structural de-risking from the AADI demerger all remain supportive, and the base case still implies ~21% upside to IDR 3,050. But it is no longer a high-conviction add at current levels. Maintain Hold, harvest the dividend, and reserve fresh buying for a pullback into the IDR 2,300–2,400 add zone or a confirmed breakout above the IDR 2,700 pivot. The key catalyst to watch into year-end is the November MSCI frontier-market review, which represents the largest asymmetric risk to the position independent of ADRO's own fundamentals.
