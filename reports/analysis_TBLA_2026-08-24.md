The central catalyst has **strengthened and de-risked further** since Aug 12:
- **B50 rollout progress is now independently confirmed**: as of mid/late-August, roughly **57% of Indonesian gas stations were already distributing B50** fuel, up from partial coverage at the July 1 launch, with the figure "expected to continue increasing" toward the October 1 nationwide-compliance target (Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources reporting). - **Residual caveat, narrowed but not closed:** ~43% of stations still await B50 conversion with five weeks to the Oct 1 deadline — a tight but not implausible runway. This is now the primary execution risk to monitor, replacing the broader policy-uncertainty risk that dominated prior reports.

### Stress Tests

#### BI Rate Path — the critical scenario for TBLA

**(a) Realized: BI held at 5.75% (Aug 18–19) — the dominant Aug 12 risk did not fire.** With D/E at 193% and interest coverage at ~1.9x, the previously-modeled 50bps hike to 6.25% would have added an estimated Rp70–90bn of incremental annual interest expense. That hit was avoided this cycle. This is the single largest driver of the improved thesis since Aug 12.

**(b) New scenario: BI cuts 25–50bps (5.75%→5.25–5.50%), consistent with emerging sell-side commentary under the new Acting Governor.** **Positive, moderate-to-high impact.** A cut of this size would reduce annual interest expense by a similar Rp35–90bn range (mirroring the hike math in reverse), directly improving both net profit and the ~1.9x coverage ratio — a second, independent re-rating catalyst layered on top of the B50 volume story. Not yet the base case, but now a live possibility rather than a tail scenario.

**(c) Residual tail risk: a delayed hike later in 2026** (e.g., a Fed policy reversal, a renewed IDR sell-off, or an inflation surprise) remains possible despite the new Acting Governor's dovish framing — central bank leadership transitions can also reverse. If it occurred, the impact would be materially the same as modeled Aug 12 (high-single-digit to low-teens percentage hit to net profit). Probability is now assessed as lower than on Aug 12, but not zero — this remains the single largest identified risk to the thesis in magnitude, even if its near-term likelihood has fallen.

#### IDR Strengthens to ~17,665 (realized) / Stress Case: IDR Weakens to 19,500

The realized move this window was **favorable** — IDR strengthened from 17,870 to ~17,665 (Aug 21), consistent with the new Acting Governor's stated policy goal. As a forward stress case, an IDR weakening to 19,500 (~9% depreciation from the Aug 12 base) would still carry the same mixed impact flagged previously: TBLA's partial natural hedge (USD-referenced CPO/biodiesel revenue against USD-linked working capital/import costs) limits but doesn't eliminate the drag. Net estimated EPS impact: −3% to −5%, unchanged from Aug 12. Probability of this scenario has fallen given the realized strengthening trend.

#### MSCI Frontier Downgrade — November 2026 Review

Unchanged from Aug 12: minimal direct impact. TBLA is a small/micro-cap not held in MSCI Indonesia Standard/EM baskets — a reclassification would not trigger direct passive selling. Indirect risk runs through broad IHSG liquidity/sentiment rather than mechanical index outflows — still a second-order risk for this position.

## Final Verdict: 🟡 Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN, Conviction Raised

TBLA remains a **leveraged, policy-catalyst special situation**, not a defensive GARP holding — but the two weeks since Aug 12 have moved the thesis in the right direction on every axis that matters most. The dominant flagged risk, a hawkish Bank Indonesia rate path, did not materialize: the BI Rate held at 5.75% for a second straight month on August 18–19, the first decision under new Acting Governor Destry Damayanti, who explicitly guided toward further rupiah strength rather than the 6.25% year-end hike scenario this series had been pricing. Simultaneously, CPO prices hit a 20-month high and Indonesia's B50 rollout is now independently confirmed at ~57% station coverage, tracking toward the October 1 nationwide-compliance deadline — concretely narrowing the execution-risk caveat flagged in the last report. Against this improving backdrop, TBLA's own share price gave back ~7.7% while the IHSG rallied to a fresh high, opening a wide fundamentals-outrunning-price gap and pushing risk/reward from 2.48:1 to 5.25:1. The balance sheet itself is unchanged (D/E 193%, coverage ~1.9x) — this is a reprieve on the *trigger* for that risk, not a repair of the risk itself, so position sizing should stay disciplined. We maintain Speculative Buy, raise the base target modestly to IDR 1,080, and hold the hard stop flat at IDR 580 — now a materially tighter, more meaningful line given the price pullback. This remains a high-conviction, high-volatility satellite position, not a core allocation, but the risk/reward has rarely looked better within this name's own history in this series.
