## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — UNCHANGED

The week of Sept 21–25 was a low-conviction continuation of the post-breakout drift, but with a genuinely mixed set of new data points rather than a simple extension of the prior week's weakness. On the encouraging side: foreign net-selling decelerated sharply (from -Rp169.7bn to -Rp78.3bn week-on-week, even as market-wide foreign outflows intensified), the stock bounced +1.56% off a Sept 24 low to close the week at 2,600, and the rupiah's continued slide to ~17,900–17,950 turned a three-report-long hypothetical tailwind into an actual, if still moderate, one. On the more cautious side: this is now a third consecutive week of net foreign selling in ADRO specifically, and a newly-surfaced medium-term Newcastle coal forecast (Q4 2026 averaging ~$127/t, easing to ~$116/t through 2027) introduces a genuine headwind to the Bull-case DCF assumption that this series has not previously had to weigh, even though spot pricing itself hasn't moved much. Nothing in the core fundamental case changed: the KAI aluminum smelter remains on track for Sept–Oct 2026 full capacity, H1 2026 earnings remain the latest confirmed print with Q3 not yet due, and the BI governance transition passed its first test (the Sept 22–23 RDG) without incident. Net effect on the scorecard: a one-point improvement to 48/70, with Momentum ticking up on the deceleration/bounce, R/R improving mechanically on the cheaper entry, and Growth ticking down one point to reflect the new coal-forecast headwind to the Bull case. This remains a Speculative Buy — not a stronger conviction call (momentum needs to reconfirm above 2,660 and ultimately 2,840–2,860 before that's warranted) and not a weaker one (nothing fundamental broke, and the stock remains well clear of its 2,450 stop). Hold existing positions, treat the 2,550–2,620 zone as the area to add on weakness per this window's desk commentary, and watch the November MSCI review and any further Newcastle coal forecast revisions as the two variables most likely to move this call next.
