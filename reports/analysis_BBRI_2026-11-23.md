## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

**Efficiency (inflection):** 9M 2026 profit Rp48.2tn (+16.8%), ROE 19.2% vs ~17% a year ago, NPL down to 2.9%. This is the first year-on-year ROE gain after the 2024-25 micro-loan credit-cost cycle. **Inflection: yes, but driven by lower provisions.** Core NII is flat-to-down, and the rate hike works against it.

**Growth drivers (next 2 quarters):** (1) loan growth ~14.5%; (2) the reserve-requirement (GWM) cut from Nov 1 frees liquidity; (3) credit costs still normalising; (4) dividend season. **New headwind:** a 25bp policy hike raises deposit costs faster than micro-loan yields can reprice. RHB flags margin pressure if deposit rates move first.

**Cash strength:** bank, so FCF is not meaningful. Capital and dividend capacity are strong; no dilution risk.
