## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

This is the crux of the call. GPRA screens as absurdly cheap on every static multiple (P/B 0.31–0.33x, sell-side NAV discount ~80%), yet:

1. **This is not a new discount.** GPRA-type small-cap Indonesian developers have traded at 0.25x–0.50x book for years without the gap closing — the discount is structural (illiquidity, thin free float, weak institutional coverage, historical insider share sales in 2025), not a temporary mispricing a catalyst will fix.
2. **Two consecutive years of profit decline** (FY2025 -31.84% YoY, Q1 2026 -82.8% YoY) confirm this is not merely cyclical noise — core landed-housing revenue is genuinely shrinking (-39.1% YoY in Q1 2026).
3. **The Q2 2026 rebound is real but unverified as high-quality.** Aggregator data show Q2 2026 revenue of ~Rp121.9bn (+50.6% QoQ) and net profit of ~Rp24.2bn (vs Rp6.4bn in Q1) — we could not confirm this against a primary IDX filing (access blocked), so treat it as directionally credible but not fully audited by us. Even taking it at face value, H1 2026 net profit of ~Rp30.6bn remains well below the pace implied by Q1 2025 alone (Rp37.3bn), i.e. **H1 2026 is still down meaningfully YoY.**
4. **Sector-wide demand data corroborates the bear case**, not the bull case: national home sales -25.7% YoY, mortgage growth decelerating to 5.1% YoY, mortgage NPLs rising to 3.2%, and BI having hiked (not cut) 100bps in H1 2026 — the opposite of the easing cycle a housing recovery thesis would need.
5. **The rights issue is a wildcard that cuts against the value case**: raising ~Rp300bn to acquire three undisclosed property companies, funded via dilutive equity issuance at a depressed share price, risks locking in the discount rather than closing it — especially absent disclosed valuation/accretion terms.

**Conclusion: structural decline with a QoQ earnings bounce, not a reversal.** The valuation discount is a symptom of chronic illiquidity and weak capital allocation signaling, not an exploitable mispricing.

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

### Risk/Reward

No actionable R/R is computed under an Avoid rating. For reference: downside to the Bear DCF (~75) or stop level (90) is -19.6% to -33%; upside to Base DCF (~115) is a marginal +2.7%; Bull-case upside (+56% to ~175) requires unconfirmed assumptions. The skew is asymmetric toward the downside in the base/bear cases, which is disqualifying for a constructive rating regardless of the deep-value optics.

### Stress Tests

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500 — Negative, imported materials
A further ~9% rupiah depreciation (from ~17,900 today) would compound the construction wholesale price index pressure already running +8.1% YoY (Apr-26, vs +0.8% a year prior), driven partly by rupiah weakness and oil prices. Gross margin (currently ~55–58%) could compress toward 48–50%, disproportionately hitting the Nemuru hotel build-out (imported FF&E) and pushing the Bear DCF scenario closer to the realized outcome.

#### BI Rate +50bps — Severe, kills mortgage demand
A move to 6.25% from 5.75% would land on top of mortgage repricing already expected in H2 2026, likely pushing effective commercial (non-subsidized) KPR rates toward 13%+. Given mortgage growth has already slowed to 5.1% YoY and bad-loan ratios are climbing (3.2% Apr-26), this would materially threaten GPRA's Rp500–550bn 2026 marketing sales target and would likely tip the base case toward the Bear DCF outcome (~75/share).

#### MSCI Frontier Downgrade Nov 2026 — Minimal, already Avoid
GPRA's micro market cap (~USD 27mn) and thin free float mean it was never an MSCI Indonesia constituent — a hypothetical Emerging-to-Frontier downgrade would hit large-cap index names and passive flows far more than GPRA directly. Indirect risk-off contagion to IHSG small-caps is possible but is already reflected in the cautious stance; this scenario changes little at the position level.

## Final Verdict: 🔴 Avoid — MAINTAIN

The thesis from July 4 is essentially intact, with one genuine positive data point and one genuine new risk that roughly offset. On the positive side, Q2 2026 results (per secondary/aggregator data, not independently verified against a primary filing) show a sharp QoQ recovery in both revenue (+50.6%) and net profit (~+280%), and the stock continues to screen as extraordinarily cheap against sell-side NAV estimates (~80% discount). On the negative side, H1 2026 profit is still down materially versus H1 2025, BI has hiked rates 100bps this year with mortgage repricing still working through the system, national home sales remain deeply negative (-25.7% YoY), mortgage credit quality is deteriorating, and management has now layered on a dilutive rights issue to fund an unspecified M&A plan. The 52-week-high land-bank-discount story has been true for years without closing — this is the textbook signature of a value trap, not a mispricing. We raise the score modestly (20→23/70) to reflect the earnings inflection and continue to recommend legacy holders manage risk into strength, using a trailed stop-loss reference of IDR 90, while withholding any fresh capital commitment. Re-evaluate at Q3 2026 results and upon clarity on the rights-issue terms/use of proceeds, expected Q4 2026.
