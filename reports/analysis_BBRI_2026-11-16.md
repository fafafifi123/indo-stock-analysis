## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

**Efficiency (inflection):** ROE 19.2% vs 17% a year ago: the first year-on-year improvement after the 2024-25 micro-loan credit-cost cycle. NPL down to 2.9%. **Inflection: yes, but provision-led** — core NII is flat-to-down, so the quality of the inflection is moderate.

**Growth drivers (next 2 quarters):** (1) loan growth 14.5% with cheaper funding (management: "strengthen low-cost funds before credit expansion"); (2) GWM ratio cut from Nov 1 frees liquidity; (3) credit-cost normalisation continuing; (4) interim dividend season. Risk: NIM compression (Stockbit) and dividend-payout normalisation.

**Cash strength:** bank — FCF not meaningful. Capital and dividend capacity are strong; no dilution risk.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

**Value-trap check:** not structural. The Oct 16-19 collapse to 2,540 (-18% in two sessions) was flow-driven (MSCI fear, foreign selling) and fully reversed in four weeks. Loan-book growth and falling NPL contradict a structural-decline story.

**Hidden assets:** ultra-micro holding (Pegadaian, PNM), BRI Insurance, BRI Finance — carried, not re-priced.
