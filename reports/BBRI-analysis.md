## 1. Quality Filter

### Profitability & Capital Efficiency

| Metric | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024/TTM | Trend |
|--------|------|------|------|------|----------|-------|
| ROE | ~13% | ~14% | ~18% | ~21% | ~18% | ⬇️ Declining |
| Net Profit Margin | ~24% | ~32% | ~42% | ~46% | ~40% | ⬇️ Declining |
| Revenue (IDR T) | ~97T | ~106T | ~118T | ~128T | ~134T | ✅ Growing |
| Net Income (IDR T) | ~23T | ~34T | ~50T | ~58T | ~55T | ⚠️ Peaked |

**ROE Assessment:** BBRI's ROE peaked at ~21% in FY2023 and has since declined to ~18% as micro-loan provisions surged. The 5-year CAGR for earnings is ~18.4%, impressive over the full cycle, but the recent negative momentum (-5.5% YoY) is concerning. For a bank, ROE >15% is the threshold for capital efficiency — BBRI still passes, but the direction is wrong.

**ROIC (Bank Equivalent — Return on Assets):** ROA is approximately 3.2-3.5% (estimated), strong for an Indonesian bank. BBRI earns substantially above its cost of deposits.

### Economic Moat

**Moat Type: Wide Moat (but under stress)**

BBRI has a genuine and durable franchise in Indonesia's micro and small-medium enterprise (MSME) lending segment — the largest in the country by volume. Key moat sources:
- **Distribution network:** 10,000+ Mantri (field loan officers) embedded in rural communities; BRILink agent network with 1M+ outlets
- **Government backing:** State-owned bank, implicit sovereign support; access to low-cost government deposits
- **Brand in micro segment:** Synonymous with rural Indonesia banking; brand loyalty extremely high

**Moat challenge (current):** The micro segment — 44.7% of BBRI's loan book (IDR 632.8T) — is experiencing stress following post-COVID normalization and the economic slowdown effect on rural households. NPL in micro reached 3.86% (+90bps YoY), and Special Mention Loans (SML) sit at 6.76%. A large write-off occurred in late 2024.

**Growth Catalysts:**
- Rural financial inclusion — Indonesia still has ~50M unbanked adults
- Digital super-app (BRImo) reaching 38M+ users; digital transaction volume growing rapidly
- Government policy alignment — BBRI is a key vehicle for Kredit Usaha Rakyat (KUR) distribution
- Recovery in micro segment post-write-off (2025-2026 expected normalization)

## 2. Governance & Risk

### Corporate Governance (GCG)
- **Board structure:** Standard SOE governance with Ministry of SOE oversight
- **Related-party transactions:** Limited and disclosed — typical SOE operational relationships
- **Audit quality:** Big 4 auditor (Purwantono, Sungkoro & Surja / EY affiliate)
- **Dividend policy:** ~70-80% payout ratio, consistent — no governance red flags
- **GCG Red Flag Score: LOW** — BBRI has a clean governance track record for a state bank

### Value Trap Assessment

**Is BBRI a value trap?**

Arguments against value trap:
1. Core franchise in micro lending remains intact — the brand and distribution network are real, durable assets
2. The NPL blowup appears cyclical (economic slowdown) not structural (fraud, mismanagement)
3. Conservative provisioning — the late-2024 write-off was large but cleaned up the balance sheet
4. 5-year earnings CAGR of 18.4% despite the current dip

Arguments for caution:
1. Micro segment stress may persist longer than expected (1-3Q 2026)
2. NIM compression as funding costs rise
3. Fintech disruption in the micro/informal segment (e.g., GoPay, OVO, fintech lending)

**Verdict:** NOT a value trap. BBRI is a cyclically-stressed quality bank, not a structurally declining one. The risks are priced in at current levels.
