## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN (Thesis Intact, But Now Testing the Stop-Loss Line)

The two weeks since Sept 14 produced no new hard fundamental data — Q3 2026 results remain ~4-5 weeks out — but they did produce genuine confirmation of macro risks that were only probabilistic before, and a meaningful stress-test of this series' key supporting evidence. The Fed's 25bp hike (Sept 16) went from an 80-90% probability to a realized fact with hawkish forward guidance; the US 10-year Treasury yield spiked to a post-2007 high of 5.11% on Sept 25; IDR weakened further to ~17,900-17,980; and Indonesian banking stocks were explicitly singled out as the weakest cohort in the market on both Sept 24 and Sept 25, with BBRI closing at IDR 3,140 (-4.0% from Sept 11's 3,270). Bank Indonesia's hold at 5.75% under newly-confirmed permanent Governor Destry Damayanti was a clean, as-expected resolution of a prior open risk. The more consequential change is that BBRI's own foreign-flow behavior, which had been the strongest evidence this series cited for a "macro-beta, not stock-specific" read, reversed this cycle: BBRI was the exchange's second-largest net-sell stock for the week of Sept 21-25 (~-IDR 707.9 billion), a genuine break from the net-buying pattern seen Sept 7-11. With FY2026E EPS and all three price targets held unchanged and only the entry price down, the mechanical Base-case R/R computes to an implausible ~20:1 — a distortion of the framework caused by the stop now sitting just 1.3% below spot, with the Bear target and the Stop Loss having converged to the same IDR 3,100 level. We treat that as a risk-management warning, not a reason to add aggressively, and cut the score to 46/70 (from 52/70) — driven by Momentum, Macro Fit, and the quality (not the raw magnitude) of the Risk/Reward setup. We hold the stop at IDR 3,100 and all three targets (Base 3,950, Bull 4,600, Bear 3,100) unchanged, and keep the position at Speculative Buy MAINTAIN: nothing in the fundamental thesis — H1 loan growth, S&P's Sept 8-9 rating affirmation, improving loan-at-risk — has broken, and this week's price action still looks sector-wide and macro-driven rather than BBRI-specific. But the margin for error has compressed to its thinnest point in this series, and a close below IDR 3,100 should be treated as a clean stop-out trigger rather than a dip to average into.
