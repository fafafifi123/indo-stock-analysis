## 1. The "Gem" Quality & Growth Filter

### 3-Year ROE & ROIC Trend — Inflection Analysis

| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | H1 2025 | Inflection? |
|--------|------|------|------|---------|-------------|
| Revenue (IDR T) | ~16T | ~15.4T | ~17.5T | — | ✅ Growing (biodiesel) |
| Net Income (IDR B) | ~600B | ~611B | ~700B | — | ✅ Growing |
| Net Margin | ~3.8% | ~4.0% | ~4.0% | ~5%+ est. | ✅ Slow improvement |
| ROE | ~9% | ~9% | ~9.5% | — | 🔴 BELOW cost of equity |
| Interest Coverage | ~2.0x | ~1.8x | ~1.9x | — | 🔴 DANGEROUSLY LOW |
| D/E Ratio | ~175% | ~185% | ~193% | — | 🔴 WORSENING |

**Critical Finding — ROE vs Cost of Equity:**
TBLA's ROE of 9.5% is BELOW its estimated cost of equity of ~12-13%. This means the company is **destroying economic value** at current profitability levels. Every IDR of shareholder equity is earning less than shareholders require for the risk taken. This is the fundamental disqualification for "gem" status.

**The Biodiesel Exception:** H1 2025 biodiesel revenue surged +173% YoY (IDR 1.5T → IDR 4.1T). If this rate continues into FY2025, net income could reach IDR 1.0-1.2T, pushing ROE toward 12-15% — this is the ONLY scenario that makes TBLA investable, and it depends entirely on government policy.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **B40 → B50 Mandate Progression:** Indonesia's biodiesel blending ratio moving from B40 (Jan 2025) toward B50 (planned 2026). TBLA's FY2025 biodiesel contract = 809,000 KL (2x FY2024). If B50 mandated, volume roughly doubles again.
2. **Biodiesel Contract Renewals (Q3 2026):** Annual government biodiesel contracts are the critical catalyst. FY2026 contract announcement will set expectations. Any positive volume surprise = stock re-rates.
3. **CPO Price Recovery:** TBLA's margins are leveraged to crude palm oil (CPO) prices. If CPO rises toward $1,000/MT (vs ~$800-850 current), gross margin expands meaningfully.

**CRITICAL RISK ON CATALYSTS:** All three catalysts are POLICY-DEPENDENT or COMMODITY-DEPENDENT. They are completely outside management control. This is NOT a quality business catalyst — it is a bet on government decisions and commodity prices.

### FCF / Cash Strength — SEVERE WARNING

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Net Margin | 4.0% | 🔴 Razor-thin; any commodity swing = profit wiped |
| Operating Cash Flow | NEGATIVE (per recent reports) | 🔴 CRITICAL — profit not converting to cash |
| D/E Ratio | 193.2% | 🔴 Debt is nearly 2x equity |
| Interest Coverage | 1.9x | 🔴 MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE IS 3x |
| Dividend Yield | ~3-4% | 🟡 Minimal given risks |

**FCF is NEGATIVE despite reported net profit.** This is a major red flag — it means TBLA is booking accounting profit but consuming cash. Working capital changes, high capex, and debt service are absorbing all cash generation. A company with negative operating cash flow while reporting profit is exhibiting classic earnings quality deterioration.
