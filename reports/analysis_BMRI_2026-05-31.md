| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | Q1 2026 (TTM) | Trend |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|---------------|-------|

**Inflection Assessment:** BMRI shows no inflection — it is a *consistent compounder*. ROE has climbed every year for 5 consecutive years. The Q1 2026 ROE of 22.1% is the highest on record. Unlike BBRI (under NPL stress), BMRI's asset quality is pristine (NPL 0.41% net vs. BBRI's 4.15% micro NPL). This is the cleaner bank in the large-cap universe.

**ROIC (Bank Equivalent — RAROC):** BMRI's Risk-Adjusted Return on Capital comfortably exceeds its ~10% cost of equity. CASA ratio of 71.6% provides extremely low-cost funding, widening the spread.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **Digital fee income explosion:** Livin' app fee income grew +19.8% YoY in Q1 2026. With 40M+ Livin' users, the shift from interest income to fee income de-risks the NIM compression narrative.
2. **Corporate banking dominance:** Infrastructure and corporate loan book growing at 16.2% YoY — aligned with government capex cycle (IKN, toll roads, energy transition projects).
3. **BRIS deconsolidation clarity:** With Bank Syariah Indonesia (BRIS) deconsolidated from Q1 2026, BMRI's standalone financials are now "cleaner" and easier for investors to model — a re-rating catalyst.
4. **BI rate cut cycle:** Bank Indonesia began signaling cuts in H1 2026. BMRI's asset-sensitive balance sheet benefits from rising NIM when rates are cut (floating rate loans re-price up faster than fixed deposits).

### Cash Strength & FCF

- BMRI generates net profit of ~IDR 15.4T per quarter (annualised IDR 61.6T)
- CAR: 19.7% (far above 12% minimum) — enormous buffer to grow without equity dilution
- **FCF Yield: ~4.1%** on current market cap — comfortably self-funding loan book growth
- Dividend yield: ~10.6% (consistent 80%+ payout ratio)
- No equity dilution risk: BMRI has not issued new shares in 5 years
