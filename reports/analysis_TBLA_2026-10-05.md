### Stress Tests

#### BI Rate +50bps (5.75% → 6.25%) — the critical scenario for TBLA
BI held on Sept 22–23, so the near-term trigger passed without a hike. A 50bp hike would add an estimated Rp70–90bn of annual interest expense (carried forward estimate; no new balance-sheet data). Against EBIT of Rp2,596bn and ~1.9x coverage this would pressure EPS (magnitude not quantified). With the Fed signalling another hike and the IDR near 17,855, the odds of a BI hawkish turn are higher than on Sept 14 (assessment, not a forecast). This remains the largest-magnitude risk.

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500
From ~17,855 (Sept 22), 19,500 is about +9.2% depreciation. TBLA's USD-referenced revenue partly hedges USD-linked costs; net estimated EPS impact -3% to -5% (carried forward). Realized IDR weakened about 1.4% since Sept 14, so the stress case is no longer remote. Dollar-denominated debt exposure was not verified.

#### CPO Price Path
Realized: BMD Oct contract fell to roughly RM4,374–4,433 (Sept 29) from RM4,814 (Sept 11). The lower end of the Sept forecast band (RM4,400–4,600) has already been reached; a break below would pressure upstream margins, while biodiesel (HIP-linked) is less exposed. No EPS-per-RM100/t sensitivity was found (qualitative, est.). Upside: a recovery above RM4,800 would restore the Sept 14 picture.

#### MSCI Frontier Downgrade — November 2026 Review
Minimal direct impact (TBLA is not in MSCI Indonesia Standard baskets; carried forward); indirect sentiment risk is higher given foreign YTD net selling above Rp82.5T and IHSG at a 2-month low.

## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN, lower conviction

The B50 thesis delivered on schedule (full implementation Oct 1), the feared BI rate shock did not happen, and TBLA held up much better than the market (-3.2% vs IHSG -7.7%). Against that, CPO has dropped roughly 8–9%, the Fed is hiking, the IDR is weaker, the stock lagged every palm peer after B50 started (a sign the market is discounting leverage, D/E ~193%, interest cover ~1.9x, unverified until Oct 30), and the stop is now just 4.1% below the price. The 19.8:1 R/R is an artifact of the tight stop. Keep this as a small satellite position, hold the 580 stop, and use the Oct 30 Q3/9M filing as the decisive check on balance sheet and B50 earnings conversion. A reclaim of 625–640 would improve the technical picture; a close below 580 would argue for exit.
