## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

**Is BMTR cheap for a reason (structural decline) or mispriced (temporary)?**

**Arguments for Value Trap:**
- FTA TV audience is structurally declining due to streaming (YouTube, TikTok, Netflix)
- Digital ad spending is shifting to social media at the expense of TV
- BMTR's digital ventures (okezone, iNews.id) have been loss-making for years
- Revenue has been flat for 3+ years = no organic growth

**Arguments Against Value Trap:**
- P/B of 0.16x at IDR 185 is extreme even for a zero-growth media company
- D/E 21.6% = company is NOT leveraged; rate hikes don't threaten the business
- RCTI and MNCTV (subsidiaries via MNCN) remain the #1 and #2 most-watched FTA TV stations in Indonesia — dominant market position
- Operating cash flow is consistently positive; company is NOT burning cash
- The "decline" has been gradual (15+ years of slow decline) — not an acute cliff

**Verdict: Partial Value Trap.** BMTR is a genuine value trap on earnings growth (flat/declining revenue) but NOT a value trap on assets (P/B 0.16x is extraordinary). The investment case is asset-based, not earnings-based. Appropriate as a small (3–5%) contrarian position, not a core holding.
