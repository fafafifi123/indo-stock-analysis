**ROE/ROIC Assessment:** BMTR's consolidated ROE of 6-8% is BELOW the cost of equity (~11-12%). The traditional media business (FTA TV advertising) is in secular decline globally as audiences migrate to digital streaming. This is the core challenge. However, the INFLECTION OPPORTUNITY lies in:

1. **Vision+ (streaming):** Subscriber growth from 6M → 17M in 3 years is a genuine digital transformation story — and it is being valued at ZERO by the market (BMTR trades at a discount to MNCN alone)
2. **Holding Company Discount Compression:** At current prices, BMTR's MNCN stake alone is worth ~IDR 5-8T depending on MNCN price — which means the market is assigning ZERO value to all other BMTR assets

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **Asian Games / Major Sporting Events 2026:** FTA TV advertising always spikes during major sporting events. Indonesia's national sports coverage on RCTI/MNCTV commands premium CPM rates from consumer goods advertisers (Unilever, Indofood, Danone). Any major event = Q3-Q4 advertising windfall.
2. **Vision+ Profitability Milestone:** Management guidance targets Vision+ EBITDA breakeven in 2026-2027. If Q2-Q3 2026 shows positive Vision+ EBITDA — or even materially reduced losses — the market will assign explicit value to the digital asset for the first time.
3. **MNC Bank / Fintech Optionality:** MNC Bank's digital banking initiatives. Small but upside optionality if bank successfully pivots to digital lending (reaching MNC's 270M-touch digital audience). Government policy supporting financial inclusion.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check — The Critical Media Company Question

| Factor | Signal | Detail |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Structural Decline? | 🔴 YES (FTA TV) | FTA TV advertising market declining 5-8% annually as audiences shift to YouTube, TikTok, Netflix |
| Revenue Declining? | 🔴 YES (MNCN) | MNCN revenue: IDR 6.8T (2022) → IDR 6.0T (2024) — declining trend |
| Digital Growth? | ✅ YES (Vision+) | Vision+ 6M → 17M subscribers in 3 years = genuine transformation |
| Governance Risk? | 🔴 HIGH | Hary Tanoesoedibjo political involvement = governance overhang |
| Balance Sheet? | 🟡 MODERATE | Holding company debt manageable; MNCN itself has net cash |

**Value Trap Risk: PARTIAL — Here is the nuanced read:**

Traditional media companies are value traps globally when they:
1. Have no digital strategy (BMTR HAS Vision+)
2. Face accelerating revenue decline (MNCN is declining but gradually, not collapsing)
3. Carry crippling debt (MNCN has net cash; BMTR holding debt is manageable)

BMTR is a "partial value trap" in that FTA TV is structurally declining, but it is NOT a FULL value trap because:
- Vision+ is growing rapidly (a genuine digital asset)
- MNCN is cash-generative even in decline
- The holding company discount provides an asset-based floor

**The Holding Company Discount = The Opportunity:**
If BMTR's MNCN stake is worth IDR 5-8T and BMTR's market cap is IDR 6.1T, the market is pricing ALL OTHER ASSETS (MNC Sky Vision, Vision+, MNC Land, MNC Bank) at approximately IDR -2T to +1T. Structurally, if Vision+ achieves even modest subscriber monetization, this discount collapses violently.

The market is paying for MNCN (declining) but getting Vision+, MNC Land, MNC Bank, and MNC Sky Vision essentially for FREE. This is the classic "holding company discount" that periodically corrects violently when a catalyst emerges (dividend, privatization, subsidiary IPO, or activist investor).

### Stress Test 1: IDR Weakens vs USD (IDR → 18,000)

- BMTR/MNCN revenues are primarily IDR (domestic TV advertising and subscriptions)
- Content acquisition costs: some USD-denominated (international shows, sports rights)
- Net impact: higher content costs compress MNCN margins → dividend to BMTR reduced
- BMTR holding debt: if denominated in IDR → minimal direct FX impact
- Foreign investor sentiment: EM risk-off → holding company discount widens further
- Price impact: IDR 430 → IDR 360-380 (-12-16%)
- **Verdict:** Manageable. The asset base (MNCN stake, land) provides floor.

### Stress Test 2: BI Raises Rates by 50bps

- BMTR holding company debt: higher interest expense directly reduces FCF to holding
- If holding debt ~IDR 2.5T at variable rate: +50bps = IDR 12.5B additional annual cost (immaterial)
- More importantly: higher rates reduce investor appetite for speculative/conglomerate stocks
- Valuation: higher discount rate compresses holding company NAV estimates by ~5-8%
- Price impact: IDR 430 → IDR 380-400 (-7-12%)
- **Verdict:** Moderate negative. BMTR is not directly rate-sensitive but is affected via valuation discount expansion.
