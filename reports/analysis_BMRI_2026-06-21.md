## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

**Is BMRI cheap because of structural decline?**

- **ROE 17.7%:** Sustained across 3 years — this is structural quality, not a cyclical spike
- **Loan Growth 13.4% FY2025:** Growing above nominal GDP — market share gains confirmed
- **CASA ~60%:** Indonesia's best wholesale bank funding franchise
- **NPL Ratio:** BMRI's NPL is significantly lower than BBRI (wholesale vs micro focus) — credit quality advantage
- **Digital transformation:** Livin' by Mandiri has 30M+ users — future cost efficiency embedded

**Verdict: NOT a value trap.** BMRI is cheap because: (1) BI rate hikes compress bank P/E multiples sector-wide, (2) MSCI risk creates forced selling uncertainty, (3) bear market indiscriminate selling. Fundamental quality is unimpaired.
