None of this reflects deteriorating quality, growth, or balance-sheet strength — those components of the score are unchanged or improved — but the combined Value and R/R compression is now large enough, and has now persisted across two consecutive reporting cycles, to pull the overall score below this series' 55-point Gem threshold for the first time (53/70 vs 57/70 on Aug 24). This is downgraded from Strong Buy to **Speculative Buy**: existing holders should continue to hold at the unchanged IDR 1,120 stop, but new capital should not chase at IDR 1,465. The two live paths to repairing this setup are unchanged in kind but more urgent in tone than on Aug 24 — a pullback into the IDR 1,300–1,350 add zone, or an October 29 Q3 print strong enough to lift the DCF target — and until one of them arrives, this is a name to monitor rather than add to.
