## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

- **Asset turnover:** ~0.84x (annualized H1 2026 revenue run-rate ~Rp23.4tn vs. total assets Rp27,975bn) — respectable for an integrated agribusiness, not deteriorating.
- **Inventory/CPO exposure:** TBLA is upstream-integrated (own plantations) *and* downstream (biodiesel, cooking oil), which partially insulates it from pure feedstock-cost squeezes that hit refining-only biodiesel peers. The current cheapness (P/E ~5.5x, P/B ~0.22x) looks **cyclical/policy-driven, not structural** — it reflects (a) a multi-year period of low sector multiples across Indonesian palm names, and (b) the market discounting execution risk on B50 timing, not a broken business model. The biodiesel offtake is government-mandated, which provides revenue visibility most commodity plays lack.
- **Risk to this view:** if B50 nationwide rollout slips materially past Oct 1 2026 (per the "maintain B40" guidance caveat above), the re-rating catalyst could stall, in which case the cheap multiple would persist rather than close — this is the key variable to monitor into Q3.

### Stress Tests

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500 (from 17,870, ~9% depreciation)
Mixed impact. TBLA carries meaningful USD-linked working capital and import costs, but also earns USD-referenced revenue via CPO/biodiesel pricing and exports — a partial natural hedge unlike pure importers. Net estimated EPS impact: –3% to –5%. Not a standalone thesis-breaker, but compounds pressure alongside the rate-risk scenario below.

#### BI Rate +50bps (5.75% → 6.25%, consistent with Danamon's year-end forecast)
**HIGH impact.** With D/E at 193% and interest coverage already thin at ~1.9x, a 50bps move on an estimated Rp14–15tn of floating/IDR-linked debt adds roughly Rp70–90bn of incremental annual interest expense — a high-single-digit to low-teens percentage hit to net profit, directly pressuring the EPS base the valuation case rests on. This is the single largest identified risk to the thesis and the primary reason the stop has only been trailed modestly (520→580) rather than loosened further.

#### MSCI Frontier Downgrade — November 2026 Review
Minimal direct impact. TBLA is a small/micro-cap not held in MSCI Indonesia Standard/EM baskets, so a reclassification would not trigger direct passive selling in the name. Indirect risk runs through broad IHSG liquidity/sentiment and further IDR weakness (see stress test 1) rather than mechanical index outflows — a second-order, not first-order, risk for this position.

## Final Verdict: 🟡 Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN

TBLA remains a **leveraged, policy-catalyst special situation**, not a defensive GARP holding. Since the July 4 report, the core B50 thesis has strengthened on the fundamentals that matter most — the June 26 allocation decree locks in TBLA's 722,000 KL 2026 contract and removes a layer of policy uncertainty, while CPO prices at 4-month highs support the upstream leg of the integrated model. The share price has moved +6.7% but has captured only a small fraction of the distance to target, keeping the risk/reward attractive at 2.48:1. The offsetting risk is unchanged in kind and heightened in probability: D/E 193%, interest coverage ~1.9x, and Bank Indonesia holding at 5.75% with a hawkish year-end bias toward 6.25% — a rate path that would meaningfully compress TBLA's earnings base. We maintain Speculative Buy, raise the base target modestly to IDR 1,050, and trail the hard stop up to IDR 580 to lock in a portion of the move while preserving room for the Oct 1 full-compliance catalyst to play out. Position sizing should continue to reflect this as a high-conviction, high-volatility satellite position, not a core allocation.
