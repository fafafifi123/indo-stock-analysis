### Stress Test 1: IDR Weakens vs USD (IDR → 18,000)

- GPRA's revenues are purely domestic IDR
- Construction costs may rise (imported materials: cement inputs, steel rebar)
- Impact: gross margin compression ~2-3% → net income ~-10%
- Price impact: IDR 122 → ~IDR 105 (-14%)
- **Mitigation:** The P/E of 4.3x means even after a 14% drop, P/E would be only ~3.7x — still absurdly cheap
- **Verdict:** Painful but not thesis-breaking at current valuation

### Stress Test 2: BI Raises Rates by 50bps

- KPR (mortgage) rates rise → buyer pool shrinks slightly for mid-market
- GPRA's upper-middle segment (IDR 1-3B units) partially insulated (cash buyers, high-income)
- Presales may slow 15-20% from peak
- Net income impact: ~-15%
- Price impact: IDR 122 → IDR 100 (-18%)
- **Key factor:** Presales growth was 45% YoY — even a 20% slowdown = still growing at 25%
- **Verdict:** Rate risk is real but moderated by the segment mix and presales lead time
