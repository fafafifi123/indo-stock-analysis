## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN (Pullback Improves the Entry; Thesis Unchanged)

The week since Sept 7 produced no new hard fundamental data — Q3 2026 results remain roughly six weeks out — but it did produce a genuinely new macro variable and a useful stress-test of the existing thesis. Markets swung to pricing an ~80-90% probability of a Fed hike at the September 15-16, 2026 FOMC meeting, on hot U.S. inflation data and hawkish commentary from Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, compounded by oil above \$107/bbl on Middle East escalation. That backdrop pulled IHSG down 1.4% over the week and dragged BBRI from IDR 3,390 to IDR 3,270 (-3.5%).
