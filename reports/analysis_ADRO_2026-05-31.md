## 1. The "Gem" Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend with Inflection Analysis

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025E | Trend |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| Revenue (USD B) | ~7.5B | ~5.2B | ~4.1B | ~3.8B | ⚠️ Declining with coal prices |
| Net Profit (USD M) | ~2,500M | ~1,200M | ~600M | ~345M | ⚠️ Declining sharply |
| ROE | ~45% | ~25% | ~14% | ~10% | ⚠️ Declining |
| ROIC | ~22% | ~12% | ~8.5% | ~8.36% | ⚠️ Near WACC |
| Dividend per share | IDR 2,850 | IDR 590 | IDR 268 | IDR 268 | ⚠️ Declining |
| Payout Ratio | ~99.96% | ~99.96% | ~99.96% | ~99.96% | ⚠️ 100% = zero reinvestment |

**No Inflection — Secular Coal Decline:**
ADRO's ROIC has fallen from 22% (FY2022 coal supercycle) to 8.36% (FY2025), barely above the estimated WACC of 8.26%. This means ADRO is creating near-zero economic profit. The dividend is essentially the entire net income — there is no retained earnings to fund growth or the alumina transformation.

**The Fundamental Tension:** ADRO is simultaneously:
1. A declining cash cow (coal producing less income each year)
2. A transformation story (alumina project expected to contribute 51.2% of profit by 2028)

The question for investors: Is the transformation fast enough, and can it fund itself without equity dilution?

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **Coal price stabilization:** Thermal coal prices have been volatile. Any upside surprise in coal prices directly benefits ADRO's near-term revenue.
2. **ADMR (metallurgical coal) ramp-up:** Hard coking coal from ADMR subsidiary is higher-margin than thermal coal — growing contribution expected.
3. **Kestrel mine Australia:** ADRO's Australian metallurgical coal operation provides geographic diversification.
4. **Alumina project (Mempawah, Kalimantan):** If the smelter-grade alumina project reaches commercial production milestones, it re-rates the stock from "coal holdco" to "mining transformation" story.

### Cash Strength & FCF

- **Dividend:** IDR 268.89/share annually; **Yield at IDR 2,530: ~10.6%**
- Payout ratio: ~99.96% — effectively all free cash flow paid out
- **Net Profit FY2025E: USD ~345M (~IDR 5.5T)**
- **Post-2025 dividend:** KB Valbury and analysts note "no dividends expected from ADRO post-2025" due to alumina capex
- **FCF Yield (before transformation capex):** ~6.8% — solid for an income stock
- **Key risk:** Alumina transformation requires massive capex (billions USD) — where does this funding come from if 100% payout continues?

**Value Trap Assessment: MEDIUM RISK for capital growth investors**
**Income Trap Assessment: LOW RISK (dividend yield is real and covered by FCF)**

ADRO is not a "value trap" for income investors — the 10.6% yield is real and paid from genuine FCF. But for growth investors hoping for capital appreciation, ADRO IS a potential trap: the coal business is in structural decline, and the alumina transformation may not deliver until 2028+.

**The "30% holdco discount" applied by analysts means:**
- ADRO's sum-of-parts is **30% above current market cap** (by analyst calculation)

- **POSITIVE for ADRO:** Revenue is USD-denominated; costs are mixed IDR/USD
- A weaker IDR means ADRO's USD coal revenue converts to MORE IDR
- Dividend in IDR terms would INCREASE
- **Assessment: Net positive** — IDR weakness is a tailwind for ADRO

**Scenario B: BI raises rates +50bps**
- ADRO has low leverage — minimal direct debt financing impact
- Discount rate rises → DCF value falls modestly
- Investor preference shifts away from high-yield stocks toward risk-free rates
- **Assessment:** Modestly negative; competition from higher deposit rates reduces ADRO's yield premium
