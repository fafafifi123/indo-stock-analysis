## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

**Efficiency (inflection):** 9M 2026 revenue Rp3.51tn (+26.1%), net profit **Rp448.6bn (+51.9%)**. Net margin ~12.8% vs ~10.6% a year ago. ROE ~43% annualised on a capital-light balance sheet. **Inflection: confirmed over two reporting periods** (H1 +54.6%, 9M +51.9%). The new FY target of Rp560bn equals roughly +40% for the full year.

**Growth drivers (next 2 quarters):** new contracts of ~Rp3.8tn to September (EmitenNews) against a full-year target of ~Rp4.0tn; data-centre and private building backlog; Q4 billing season. Risk: a new CEO may reset guidance; rupiah-driven cost inflation.

**Cash strength:** cash ~Rp860bn, **zero debt**. The interim dividend (Rp136.4bn) was paid from cash. Growth is self-funded; no dilution.
