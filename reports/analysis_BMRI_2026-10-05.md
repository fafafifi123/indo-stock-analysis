### Stress Tests

#### IDR Weakens to 19,000
From ~17,882, 19,000 is ~+6.3% depreciation (was ~9%). The Sept 14 report called the rupiah "resilient"; that no longer holds: IDR weakened ~1.5-2% (17,600-17,655 -> 17,855-17,966 range) and nears 18,000, with BI relying on intervention/hedging incentives rather than rates. BMRI's currency-matched book limits direct FX P&L risk (*carried forward* reasoning), but the main transmission is foreign flow: in this window IDR weakness and foreign selling coincided. Our Bear case (3,700) is the proxy for a severe IDR-driven outflow. Probability of the scenario is higher than on Sept 14.

#### BI Rate +50bps (to 6.25%)
BI held 5.75% on Sept 22-23 (DF 4.75%, LF 6.50%) and headline coverage says no hike signalled for 3 months. A +50bps hike would require an IDR shock. Mechanism (*carried forward*): a hike is mildly positive near-term for BMRI given CASA-heavy funding (Aug CASA composition: savings Rp563tn + demand Rp623tn of Rp1,687tn DPK, ~70%), with credit-side offsets lagging 1-2 quarters; a rate-hike-driven IDR defence would likely coincide with equity outflows, so the price effect is ambiguous. No quantified price impact is provided (not verifiable); stress read: Bear case 3,700.

#### MSCI Frontier Downgrade — November 2026
No new information found; ~50% probability *carried forward, unverified*. Foreign selling is already running since Sept 21, and some may be pre-positioning (inference, unverified). A negative November outcome would likely take BMRI through the 3,900 stop toward the Bear case; a clean outcome would be the main route to the Bull case. This remains the single event most likely to invalidate the stop.

## Final Verdict: Hold — DOWNGRADE (Strong Fundamentals, Weak Flow)

BMRI fell 7.6% from IDR 4,360 to IDR 4,030 since Sept 14 (about 1.5 points of that is the Rp66 ex-dividend), as IHSG dropped 7.7% to 6,036.89. Foreign investors sold roughly IDR 1.1tn in the week to Sept 25 and about IDR 730bn more in Sept 28-Oct 2 (approx.), making BMRI the most persistently sold large-cap; the Sept 14 hope that the prior week's selling was a pause did not hold. The Fed hiked to 3.75-4.00% with one more hike signalled; BI held 5.75% under new Governor Destry; IDR slid to ~17,880-17,970; Brent topped $100; and the Oct 1 cabinet reshuffle pushed IHSG to a 2-month low. The old 4,050 stop is breached. Offsetting this, the fundamentals are stronger, not weaker: Aug bank-only profit Rp37.5tn (+22.3%), NPL 1.0%, coverage 240.6%, loans +17.6%, and domestic institutions bought Rp857bn in the latest week — a possible accumulation signature, unconfirmed. The stock trades ~5.6x P/E and ~0.98x P/B (both estimated; below -2SD), with a trailing yield of ~11.8%. We cut the 6M Base target to IDR 5,150 (P/B 1.25x; in line with the ~5,206 consensus), Bull to 5,950, Bear to 3,700, and set the stop at 3,900 beneath the 3,940 spring low. R/R is 8.6:1 on the tight stop (3.4:1 vs Bear). Restoring Speculative Buy needs: a daily close above ~4,110, at least a week of neutral-to-positive foreign flow, and a Q3 print confirming growth and margins. Triggers for further downside: a close below 3,940, IDR above 18,000 on a sustained basis, or an adverse MSCI signal in November.
