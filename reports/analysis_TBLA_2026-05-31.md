**INFLECTION — B50 Policy Catalyst:**
Q1 2026 net profit of IDR 275B is the strongest quarterly result in 3 years. The key driver: biodiesel segment contributing 45-47% of total sales. The government's B50 mandate starting July 1, 2026 creates the most significant structural demand boost for TBLA since the B30 mandate in 2020.

**FAME production volume exploded 113% YoY in H1 2025** — TBLA is positioned as a major biodiesel supplier with 722,000 kiloliter delivery contracts for FY2026.

However, the ROE trend remains pedestrian (est. ~10-12%) and the D/E of 193% is a persistent structural concern. This is NOT a quality compounder — it is a **policy-driven cyclical play**.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters (H2 2026)

1. **B50 mandate (July 1, 2026):** Government mandated 50% biodiesel blending starting July 2026. TBLA's FAME production capacity is well-positioned to capture increased domestic demand. With B50 vs current B35, domestic biodiesel demand could increase by 3-4 billion liters/year.
2. **Biodiesel delivery contracts:** 722,000 KL already contracted for FY2026, with potential upside if B50 adds to this.
3. **CPO price recovery:** Palm oil prices recovering in H1 2026 on supply constraints and biofuel demand. Higher CPO = higher revenue for both oil and biodiesel segments.
4. **Sugar segment:** TBLA's cane sugar operations benefit from government sugar price protection policies.

### Cash Strength & FCF (Major Concern)

- **Q1 2026 net profit: IDR 275B** — looks strong
- **FCF situation: HISTORICALLY NEGATIVE** — previous years showed negative operating cash flow
- D/E: 193.2% — total debt substantially exceeds equity
- Interest coverage: ~1.9-2.1x — dangerously thin
- **The biodiesel revenue surge is the key to positive FCF:** If B50 drives revenue growth of 20-30%, operating cash flow should turn sustainably positive
- **Dividend:** Minimal; not an income stock

**Critical FCF watch:** For TBLA to avoid value trap designation, Q2 and Q3 2026 operating cash flow MUST be positive. B50 implementation starting July makes this likely — but not guaranteed.

### The "Value Trap" Check

| Diagnostic | Assessment | Signal |

| D/E Ratio | 193% | 🔴 CRITICAL concern |
| Interest Coverage | ~2.1x | 🔴 Thin margin of safety |
| Operating Cash Flow | Historically negative | 🔴 Was critical; improving in 2026 |
| Governance score | 8/54 (very low) | 🔴 CRITICAL — Stockbit ESG |

| Biodiesel volume | +113% YoY H1 2025 | 🟢 Policy tailwind |
| B50 contracts | 722K KL for FY2026 | 🟢 Revenue visibility |

**VALUE TRAP vs. CYCLICAL TURN:** TBLA occupies the dangerous middle ground.

**Bull case (not a trap):** The D/E of 193% was built up during the CPO price downturn. With B50 driving 40-50% of revenue and CPO prices recovering, cash flow is now strong enough to service debt and begin deleveraging. Q1 2026 profit of IDR 275B (annualised IDR 1.1T) vs debt service suggests improving coverage.

**Bear case (potential trap):** If CPO prices fall again or B50 implementation is delayed/reversed, TBLA's thin interest coverage means it could face severe financial distress. The governance score of 8/54 means minority shareholders have limited protection.

**Verdict: ELEVATED value trap risk — this is a HIGH BETA position requiring strict stop-losses.**

### Stress Test

**Scenario A: IDR weakens 10% (USD/IDR to 17,500)**
- CPO prices are partially USD-linked → IDR weakening means higher IDR revenue
- TBLA's USD-denominated debt (if any) would cost more in IDR terms
- Net effect: **Modestly positive** — CPO/biodiesel revenue upside outweighs debt cost
- However, government biodiesel subsidy payments could lag if IDR crisis develops

**Scenario B: BI raises rates +50bps**
- TBLA's high leverage (D/E 193%) makes it extremely sensitive to interest rates
- An additional 50bps on IDR 6T+ of debt = ~IDR 30B additional annual interest expense
- Interest coverage of 2.1x → would fall to ~1.9x with rate hike
- **Assessment: NEGATIVE and significant** — this is the most dangerous macro scenario for TBLA
- In this case, consider reducing position to minimum or exiting above IDR 800

**Overall Stress Test Summary:**
- TBLA is the most macro-sensitive stock in this portfolio
- IDR weakness: tolerable
- Rate hikes: dangerous (D/E 193%)
- B50 delay: significant blow to bull thesis
