### Growth Catalyst: B50 from July 1, 2026

**What is B50?**
Indonesia's mandatory biodiesel blend of 50% diesel + 50% crude palm oil (CPO), implemented across all domestic diesel fuel from July 1, 2026.

**Quantitative Impact on Biodiesel Demand:**
- B40 (current): 15.6 million KL of palm oil needed annually
- B50 (from July 1): 20.1 million KL of palm oil needed annually
- **Additional demand: +4.5 million KL = +29% increase in palm oil for biodiesel**

**TBLA's Positioning:**
- TBLA operates Plantations (CPO production) and Manufacturing (biodiesel FAME, cooking oil, sugar)
- TBLA has existing FAME production capacity ready to scale
- Government mandates domestic CPO pricing for biodiesel at a regulated floor = revenue certainty
- TBLA is positioned to capture 5–8% of the additional volume increase

**Revenue Impact Estimate (H2 2026):**
- Additional TBLA volume: 225,000–360,000 KL from B50 increment
- At blended revenue of ~IDR 8,000–10,000/KL → additional IDR 1.8–3.6T H2 revenue
- At ~4–5% net margin → additional net profit IDR 72–180B in H2 2026 alone
- Full year FY2026E net profit: IDR 1.2–1.5T (vs IDR 900B in FY2025 = +33–67% growth)
