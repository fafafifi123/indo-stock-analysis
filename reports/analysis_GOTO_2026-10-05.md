The relevant downside here is the IDR 24 stop (-20%) and the IDR 17 Bear (-43%, cash per share). Upside catalysts: Oct 14 EGM (capital reduction / treasury shares), buyback execution, a Q3 profit print.

The mechanism this series tracked for six weeks fired on Sept 28 and delivered the downside case: GOTO fell from IDR 50 to IDR 28 (-44%) in four sessions on a ~1-billion-lot sell queue and ~Rp979bn of foreign net selling, then bounced +7.14% to IDR 30 on Oct 2 on record exchange-wide turnover. We keep Speculative Buy but are explicit that (a) one rebound day is not a confirmed low — the Rp24–25 bid wall and the IDR 24 stop define the risk; (b) the Base/Bull targets (76/108) are carried-forward DCF outputs not corroborated by sell-side near-term targets (Rp32–38); and (c) the DCF has not been refreshed for the Fed hike. Existing holders: the old IDR 38 stop is gone — use IDR 24. New capital: stage entries and size for a plausible retest of IDR 24–26. Watch the Oct 14 EGM, buyback disclosures, and the Q3 print (~late Oct/early Nov).
