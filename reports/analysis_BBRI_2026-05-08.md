## 1. The "Gem" Quality & Growth Filter

### 3-Year ROE & ROIC Trend — Inflection Analysis

| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | Q1 2026 | Inflection? |
|--------|------|------|------|---------|-------------|
| ROE | ~18% | ~21% | ~18% | ~17% | ⬇️ Declining (cyclical) |
| Net Profit Margin | ~42% | ~46% | ~40% | ~38% | ⬇️ Peaked & declining |
| Net Income (IDR T) | ~50T | ~58T | ~55T | — | ⚠️ Peaking out |
| EPS Growth (YoY) | +21% | +16% | -5.5% | — | 🔴 Negative |

**Inflection Read:** BBRI is in a NEGATIVE inflection driven by the micro-loan NPL cycle — micro NPL hit 3.86% (+90bps YoY). The key question is whether this is the *worst* quarter or if deterioration continues. Base case: micro NPL peaks Q2-Q3 2026 and earnings inflect positively H2 2026. This is a BUY AHEAD OF THE POSITIVE INFLECTION play, not a buy-at-the-peak play.

**Why it's still investable:** BBRI's 5-year earnings CAGR is 18.4%. The current dip is a temporary cyclical correction, not the end of the franchise. The BRILink network (1M+ agents) and BRImo app (38M+ users) remain structurally intact.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **Micro NPL Stabilization (Q2-Q3 2026):** Significant write-offs in late 2024 already cleaned the book. NPL formation rate is expected to decelerate. First sign of NPL plateau = 15-20% re-rating trigger.
2. **BI Rate Cuts:** Every 25bps cut reduces BBRI's funding cost (heavy deposit base) and reduces NPL formation in the micro segment (debt service capacity improves for borrowers).
3. **KUR (Kredit Usaha Rakyat) Expansion:** Government allocates IDR 300T+ in KUR targets for FY2026. BBRI is the primary KUR distribution channel, locking in government-guaranteed loan growth.

**Risk/Reward Ratio:** 33% upside ÷ 11% downside = **3.0 : 1** ✅

### Stress Test 1: IDR Weakens vs USD (IDR → 18,000)

- Rural borrowers in IDR: minimal direct FX impact
- BBRI funding cost rises (dollar-denominated bonds)
- Impact on NPL: indirect — higher cost of living for micro borrowers
- Net income impact: ~-3-5% (BBRI mostly domestic operations)
- Price impact: IDR 3,160 → ~IDR 2,900 (-8%)
- **Verdict:** Limited direct FX exposure; dividend yield floor provides strong support

### Stress Test 2: BI Raises Rates by 50bps

- KUR rates are capped by government — not affected by BI rate
- Deposit funding costs rise → NIM compresses ~15-20bps further
- Net income impact: ~-7-10% → profit ~IDR 50T
- NPL may worsen slightly (higher borrower debt service burden)
- Dividend: IDR 250-260 → yield 8-8.2% at IDR 3,160 (still historically high)
- **Verdict:** -10-15% downside in this scenario. Manageable given current valuation.
