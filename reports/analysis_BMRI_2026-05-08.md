**Inflection Read:** BMRI is NOT at an inflection — it is at a sustained peak. ROE >20% for 3 consecutive years is exceptional for any bank globally. The "inflection" story is forward-looking: NIM compressed to 4.8% in Q1 2025 from 5.3% in Q4 2024. When BI rate cuts materialize (base case H2 2026), NIM re-expansion will create an EARNINGS INFLECTION — and the market has not priced this yet.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **BI Rate Cut Cycle (H2 2026):** Every 25bps BI cut expands BMRI's NIM by ~8-12bps. Consensus expects 50-75bps of cuts by year-end → NIM recovery from 4.8% → 5.1% adds ~IDR 3-4T to net interest income.
2. **Livin' Super-App Monetization:** Fee income growing +19.8% YoY. 38M+ users. Q2-Q3 2026 expect launch of new embedded insurance and investment products that could add IDR 1-1.5T annually in fee income.
3. **Corporate Loan Growth:** BMRI financing Indonesia's infrastructure, energy transition, and data center capex cycle. Loan book grew +16-17% (excl. BRIS). This pipeline is 12-18 months in duration — contracted revenue already on the books.

**Scenario:** USD/IDR moves from 16,240 to 18,000 (+11% IDR depreciation)
- Impact: BMRI's USD-denominated liabilities rise (trade finance, FX bonds)
- Foreign investor selling pressure (EM currency risk)
- Net income impact: ~-5% (manageable; BMRI has natural FX hedging via USD loans to exporters)
- Price impact: IDR 4,510 → IDR 4,100 (estimated -9%)
- **Verdict:** Manageable. BMRI's domestic franchise buffers FX volatility.

### Stress Test 2: BI Raises Rates by 50bps

**Scenario:** Bank Indonesia surprises with +50bps hike (against consensus)
- NIM compresses further: 4.8% → 4.4-4.5%
- Net income impact: ~-8-10% → net profit ~IDR 50T
- EPS: ~IDR 540; at 7x P/E → price ~IDR 3,780
- Dividend: ~IDR 350 → yield still 7.8% at IDR 4,510
- **Verdict:** 16% downside in a severe scenario. The dividend yield provides a hard floor.
