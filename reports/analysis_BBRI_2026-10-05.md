## Final Verdict: Hold — DOWNGRADE (Thesis Impaired Near-Term, Not Broken)

Since Sept 14, BBRI fell 6.1% to IDR 3,070 as IHSG dropped 7.7% to 6,036.89 on a Fed hike (3.75-4.00%), a BI hold that leaves the rupiah defended by intervention (IDR ~17,880-17,970), Brent above $100, and an Oct 1 cabinet reshuffle that drove IHSG to a 2-month low. The Sept 14 Speculative Buy rested on a macro-beta pullback evidenced by BBRI foreign buying and an R/R near 4:1. The first pillar failed: foreigners sold ~IDR 684bn of BBRI in the week ended Sept 25 and more on Oct 1-2, and the 3,100 stop was breached on Oct 2. New fundamental evidence (CASA 64.8% in August; Maybank's target and NIM cuts; modest +6.6% YTD profit growth) points to the funding-cost pressure flagged as the open item. Valuation is cheap (~7.55x TTM P/E, ~11.3% yield) and credit evidence acceptable, so this is a downgrade to Hold, not a sell. Base target IDR 3,860 (+25.7%) vs a revised 2,900 stop gives ~4.6:1 on paper, but full credit is not given for a stop moved after breach. To restore Speculative Buy: a close back above ~3,200, two consecutive weeks of neutral-to-positive BBRI foreign flow, and a Q3 print showing CASA/NIM stabilising. Worsening triggers: a sustained break below 3,000/2,900, IDR above 18,000, or an adverse MSCI signal.
