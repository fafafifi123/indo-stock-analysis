## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

**Is BBRI cheap because of structural decline or temporary cycle?**

- **NPL peaked at 7.9% and is now at 4.8%:** This is the classic credit cycle peak-and-turn. NOT structural decline — temporary NPL surge from micro/MSME segment post-COVID normalization.
- **NIM of 7.9%:** The highest NIM among Indonesian Big 4 banks. This is structural — BBRI's micro-lending focus commands premium yields that peers cannot replicate.
- **CASA 70.6%:** A genuine moat. BBRI has 100M+ BRI-linked accounts across 9,000+ branch points. This deposit franchise is irreplaceable.
- **Loan Growth 12.3% FY2025:** Not declining — still growing above nominal GDP. The business model is intact.

**Verdict: NOT a value trap.** The profit dip in FY2024–2025 was entirely caused by elevated NPL provisioning during the micro/MSME credit quality normalization. That cycle has peaked. The recovery in Q1 2026 confirms this.
