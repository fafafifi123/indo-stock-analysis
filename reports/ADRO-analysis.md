# ADRO — PT Alamtri Resources Indonesia Tbk
## Fundamental & Valuation Analysis

**Ticker:** IDX: ADRO (formerly Adaro Energy)  
**Sector:** Energy & Resources (Post-Coal Spinoff — Transformation Play)  
**Analysis Date:** May 8, 2026  
**Current Price:** ~IDR 2,550  
**Market Cap:** ~IDR 52-55 Trillion (estimated)  

> ⚠️ **Important Context:** ADRO completed a major corporate restructuring in December 2024. The thermal coal business was spun off into AADI (Adaro Andalan Indonesia Tbk), which listed separately. ADRO paid a USD 2.6 billion special dividend equivalent (~IDR 1,358/share) to shareholders who subscribed to AADI. ADRO is now "Alamtri Resources" — a holding company focused on metallurgical coal, alumina, renewable energy, and other non-thermal-coal assets.

**ROE Assessment:** ROE peaked at ~32% in 2022 (coal supercycle peak) and has normalized to ~17.8%. This is still acceptable but ROIC of 8.36% barely exceeds WACC of 8.26% — indicating that ADRO as reconstituted is marginally creating value, not compounding it. This is the key quality concern.

**ROIC vs WACC Spread:** Only ~10bps — essentially zero economic profit on invested capital. This is NOT the profile of a quality compounder.

### Economic Moat

**Moat Type: Narrow/Transitional**

Old ADRO had a genuine moat in Adaro Tutupan's low-strip-ratio, low-cost coal mine. That moat now resides in AADI. What remains in ADRO:

1. **Metallurgical coal (Adaro Minerals / AMI):** Early-stage Kestrel-equivalent asset in Kalimantan; high-quality hard coking coal. Long-term offtake deals being secured.
2. **INALUM alumina investment:** Exposure to the Indonesia bauxite-to-alumina value chain
3. **Renewable energy assets:** Early-stage solar/hydro projects
4. **Balance sheet strength:** Post-special dividend, ADRO retains substantial cash reserves

**Growth Catalysts:**
- Metallurgical coal prices more resilient than thermal; steel demand from infrastructure spending
- Indonesia's bauxite export ban driving alumina investment demand
- Energy transition projects qualifying for green finance at favorable rates
- Management track record (Garibaldi Thohir family) — operator excellence

## 2. Governance & Risk

### Corporate Governance (GCG)
- **Controlling shareholder:** PT Adaro Strategic Investment (Garibaldi "Boy" Thohir family) — 47.8% stake
- **Governance history:** Family-controlled but professional management; no significant related-party abuses reported
- **Special dividend transparency:** The AADI IPO and USD 2.6B special dividend were executed with full shareholder approval at AGMS — commendable governance
- **GCG Red Flag Score: LOW-MEDIUM** — Family control is a standard risk but the Thohir family has a strong execution record

### Value Trap Assessment

**Is ADRO a value trap? Partial risk — here's why:**

The stock looks cheap (P/E 3-7x) but:
1. **Earnings base changed dramatically** — comparing pre-AADI (full coal) to post-AADI (Alamtri) earnings is apples-to-oranges
2. **ROIC barely above WACC** — the new ADRO is still building its asset base; it hasn't proven it can compound capital efficiently yet
3. **Commodity exposure** — metallurgical coal still cyclical; alumina is a commodity; renewable energy is long-dated
4. **Transition risk** — management is executing a transformation, not just harvesting a cash cow

**Verdict:** Not a classic value trap (assets are real, cash flow is positive) but not a quality compounder either. Best characterized as a **transition-phase deep value/income stock**.

- 100% payout = no retained compounding
- Transformation uncertainty

**ADRO is best positioned as an income stock** for investors seeking a 10-14% dividend yield from a politically-connected, resource-rich Indonesian conglomerate in transition.

**Recommended Action:** HOLD if already owned for dividend income. New buyers: monitor for 6+ months to assess metcoal price trajectory and alumina project progress. Entry at IDR 2,100-2,300 would provide meaningful margin of safety.

**Avoid if:** You are seeking a quality compounder with >15% ROIC and retained earnings growth.
