**ROIC Analysis:** ADRO's ROIC of 19.80% (as reported by GuruFocus) is strong in absolute terms. **Key Insight: Met-Coal Differentiation**
The name change from "Adaro Energy" to "Alamtri Resources" reflects ADRO's strategic pivot toward metallurgical (met-coal) from thermal coal. Met-coal is NOT in the same ESG crosshairs as thermal coal — it is a critical ingredient for steel production that has no near-term green substitute. UOB Kay Hian's bullish 2026E case (+60% profit to $719M) is driven by:
1. Met-coal price recovery
2. +500 kton sales volume increase to 6.8M tons
3. Aluminium smelter (Kalimantan) ramping production
4. Larger BPI (Bhimasena Power Indonesia) contribution

### Growth Catalyst (Q2–Q3 2026)

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

**Is ADRO cheap for structural or temporary reasons?**

- **Met-coal vs thermal coal:** ADRO's pivot to met-coal is strategically sound. Met-coal has no green substitute in steel production. This is NOT a stranded asset story.
- **Asset-heavy but cash-generative:** Coal mines are capital-intensive but at current prices generate exceptional cash returns. The asset turnover is acceptable for the sector.
- **Aluminium diversification:** The smelter represents a genuine business model diversification that extends ADRO's life beyond the coal supercycle.

**ROIC Concern:** ROIC of ~19–20% is good but WACC for a coal-adjacent business is arguably 12–14% (ESG premium). True economic value creation spread may be narrow (5–7%). This limits ADRO's ability to justify GARP-style premium multiples — it deserves a value/income framing.

**Verdict: Income Play, not a Value Trap.** Strong dividend, USD revenues, met-coal focus. Hold for income; do not overpay for growth.

### Hidden Assets

| Asset | Notes | Value |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Aluminium Smelter | Kalimantan — new earnings stream | IDR 20–30T potential (not yet in market cap) |
| Met-coal reserves | 40+ years of reserves | Long-duration income asset |
| Renewable energy assets | Wind, hydro projects in early stage | Option value |
| BPI (coal power plant) | 2x660MW unit in Central Java | IDR 5–10T strategic value |

**Key Hidden Asset: Aluminium Smelter** — ADRO's integrated aluminium business (mining bauxite, refining into alumina, smelting into aluminium) is not yet fully valued by the market, which still sees ADRO primarily as a coal company. As the smelter ramps to full capacity, it could add $50–150M in annual profit that the market is underpricing.

## Final Verdict: 💰 Hold (Income Play) — MAINTAIN

ADRO is the right hold for investors who prioritize income, capital preservation, and USD exposure in this bear market. The combination of 11–19% dividend yield, USD revenue (natural IDR hedge), near-zero rate sensitivity, met-coal diversification, and hidden aluminium asset creates a compelling income thesis.

It is NOT a Gem because: (1) ROIC barely exceeds WACC on risk-adjusted basis, (2) coal has terminal decline risk long-term, (3) aluminium ramp adds uncertainty. But as a 1–2 year income and tactical USD hedge, ADRO earns its place in every portfolio.
