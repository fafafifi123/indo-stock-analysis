## 1. The "Gem" Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend with Inflection Analysis

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | Q1 2026 | Trend |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| ROE | ~18% | ~21% | ~18% | **~18.5%** | ⚠️ Declining from peak |
| Net Profit Growth | +67% | +17% | -5% | **+13.8%** | 🟢 Inflection Point! |
| Micro NPL | ~2.2% | ~3.0% | ~3.86% | **4.15%** | ⚠️ Still elevated |
| Net Downgrade to NPL | N/A | N/A | 6.57% | **4.82%** | 🟢 Improving sharply |
| BRImo Users (M) | 28M | 34M | 40M | **47.8M** | 🟢 Accelerating |

**INFLECTION POINT IDENTIFIED — Q1 2026:**
The most critical data point is the net NPL formation rate collapsing from 6.57% (Q1 2025) to 4.82% (Q1 2026). This means the rate at which micro loans turn bad is falling sharply — the crisis is **passing, not deepening**. Meanwhile Q1 2026 net profit surged +13.8% YoY (vs -11.5% in 1H 2025) — a dramatic reversal.

This is the textbook "early inflection" setup for a recovery trade. The worst is over; the market hasn't fully priced in the recovery yet.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **Micro NPL plateau confirmation:** If net NPL formation continues falling (4.82% → target 3.5%), provision charges will decline sharply, directly boosting net profit.
2. **BRImo digital banking fee income:** 47.8M users (+18.6% YoY), transaction values IDR 2,042T (+29.4%) — generating growing non-interest income.
3. **KUR (Kredit Usaha Rakyat) expansion:** Government increased FY2026 KUR target to IDR 300T; BBRI as dominant KUR distributor captures 60%+ of this volume.
4. **BI rate cut cycle:** Bank Indonesia expected to cut 50-100bps in H2 2026 — reduces NPL formation in micro segment (lower EMI burden on borrowers).

### Cash Strength & FCF

- Q1 2026 net profit: IDR 15.63T (+13.8% YoY) — annualised IDR 62.5T
- Dividend payout: ~75% ratio, implying IDR 290-310/share DPS for FY2026E
- **Dividend Yield: ~8.7-9.3%** at current price — acts as a floor
- CAR: ~23% (comfortably above minimum) — no capital raise risk
- FCF Yield: ~4.5% (estimated) — strong cash generation

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### The "Value Trap" Check

| Diagnostic | Assessment | Signal |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Micro loan growth | 1.6% YoY (stagnant) | ⚠️ Core engine still slow |
| NPL Gross | ~4.15% micro NPL | ⚠️ Elevated but improving |
| Net NPL Formation | 4.82% (down from 6.57%) | 🟢 Peak passed |
| Write-off volume | IDR 4.4T (vs 6.1T prior year) | 🟢 Lower = balance sheet healing |
| Revenue (NII + Fee) | IDR 52.84T Q1 (+1% vs forecast) | 🟢 Beats expectations |
| BRILink agent network | 1M+ agents nationwide | 🟢 Moat intact |

**Verdict: NOT a value trap — CYCLICAL RECOVERY PLAY.**

The structural franchise (rural micro banking, BRILink 1M+ agents, brand synonymous with rural Indonesia) is intact. The NPL crisis was driven by post-COVID normalization of micro borrowers — not fraud, mismanagement, or structural disruption. Fintech lending has not significantly disrupted BBRI's core customer base (bottom of pyramid, often unbanked).

### Asset Turnover & Inventory Check

For banks, equivalent metrics are:
- **Loan Book Quality (Inventory):** Writing down aggressively — IDR 6.1T write-offs in 2025, now declining to IDR 4.4T pace
- **NIM Trend:** 7.9% in Q1 2026 (beats guidance of 7.6-7.8%) — assets are earning more than expected
- **Recovery Rate:** 43.3% — recovering nearly half of all written-off loans via collection

**No structural business model deterioration detected.**

### Hidden Assets

| Asset | Estimated Value |

| **BRImo Digital Platform** (47.8M users) | Platform value not captured in book value |
| **PT BRI Danareksa Sekuritas** (subsidiary) | Profitable investment banking/brokerage arm |

BBRI's P/B of ~2.2x understates platform value of BRImo and network value of BRILink.

### Stress Test

**Scenario A: IDR weakens 10% (USD/IDR to 17,500)**
- Rural micro borrowers earn IDR — no direct FX exposure in loan book
- Risk-off may reduce equity P/E by ~0.5x → fair value drops to IDR 3,800
- Dividend yield remains >8% at IDR 3,350 → strong floor
- **Assessment: Manageable; dividend yield is the airbag**

**Scenario B: BI raises rates +50bps**
- Higher rates increase EMI burden on micro borrowers — could INCREASE NPL formation
- NIM for BBRI would be mixed (asset-sensitive, but KUR rates are capped by government)
- **Assessment: Modestly negative for BBRI** (more negative than for BMRI due to higher exposure to rate-sensitive micro borrowers)
- Downside: NPL stays elevated 2 extra quarters → delays recovery narrative
- Fair value in this scenario: IDR 3,100-3,300
