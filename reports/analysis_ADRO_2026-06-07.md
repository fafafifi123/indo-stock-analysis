**Inflection — Negative:** ROIC is converging toward and potentially below WACC. A 10bps spread (FY2025) is economically indistinguishable from zero value creation. **Why does the stock still have merit?** Because the 100% payout ratio means there is little new capital being invested at these poor ROIC levels. Cash generation is being returned to shareholders rather than reinvested. This makes it an income vehicle by design.
