## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — UNCHANGED

The week of Sept 8–14 was, in essence, a pause and partial reversal after the genuine breakout confirmed on Sept 7. ADRO/Alamtri gave back roughly 2.9% (2,720→2,640) in a week when the broader IHSG also softened (-1.43%), RSI cooled from an overbought-adjacent 60.5 to a neutral ~53.6, and foreign investors flipped from net buying (the Sept 2–3 breakout days) to net selling (an estimated IDR 90–113 billion over Sept 7–11, with genuine source-to-source variance left unreconciled rather than papered over). The net effect on the scorecard is a one-point drop to 47/70: Momentum falls from 7 to 5 to reflect the stalled breakout and reversed foreign flow, partially offset by a Risk/Reward metric that mechanically improves to 3.21:1 from 1.96:1 simply because the entry price is now cheaper against an unchanged IDR 3,250 base target and IDR 2,450 stop. That combination — a cheaper, statistically better-priced entry sitting on top of a technically softer short-term trend — is the honest picture this week, and it argues for staying at Speculative Buy rather than either upgrading (Momentum needs to reconfirm above 2,840–2,860 first) or downgrading (nothing fundamental broke; the stock remains well above its stop and the broader uptrend structure). Hold existing positions above the IDR 2,450 stop, treat a clean reclaim of 2,690–2,740 as the signal that the breakout attempt is back on, and watch the Sept 22–23 BI RDG and the November MSCI review as the two catalysts most likely to move this call further in either direction.
