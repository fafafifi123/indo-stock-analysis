## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — UPGRADED FROM HOLD

The two weeks since Aug 24 resolved the single biggest overhang this report series has carried for two consecutive editions: ADRO/Alamtri's H1 2026 results are now out, and they beat rather than merely met the guidance the sell side had been modeling — net profit up 76.8% YoY to US$309.4 million, revenue up 16.5% YoY, operating income up 87.8% YoY, on real cost discipline (COGS +0.55% YoY against 16.5% revenue growth). That confirmation landed alongside two other genuine positives: a decisive, volume-backed technical breakout through the 2,580–2,590 resistance shelf that had been flagged as an overbought sticking point on Aug 24 (the stock is now testing 2,840–2,860, a new multi-month high), and a meaningful rally in Newcastle thermal coal to ~$140–149/t from ~$130–133/t. The KAI aluminum smelter's full-capacity timeline (September–October 2026) held without slippage — itself a quiet but important de-risking, since execution risk not materializing is easy to overlook next to more dramatic news. Put together, this is the first window in this report series where fundamentals, technicals, and the commodity backdrop all improved in the same direction at the same time, and it justifies moving off Hold: the trailing P/E fell to a genuinely cheap ~7.6x on confirmed (not guidance-derived) earnings even as the price rose 5.4%, and R/R improved to 1.96:1 from 1.58:1. Two caveats keep this a Speculative Buy rather than a higher-conviction call: the stock is now up against a fresh resistance shelf (2,840–2,860) rather than sitting comfortably below one, and the November MSCI review remains an unresolved macro tail risk that could hit a large-cap, foreign-held name like ADRO disproportionately in a downgrade scenario. Initiate or add on this breakout, trail the stop to IDR 2,450, watch for a clean break of 2,840–2,860 to confirm continuation toward the IDR 3,250 base target, and treat the September 22–23 BI RDG and the November MSCI review as the two catalysts most likely to move this call further in either direction.
