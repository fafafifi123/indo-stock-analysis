This is the first genuinely constructive window in this series. TBLA's price rose ~0.8% (625→630, confirmed Sept 18 close), ending a five-checkpoint losing streak and narrowing the cumulative drawdown since Aug 12 to −11.9% (from −12.6%) — and, for the first time, this move occurred **against** a falling broad market (IHSG: 6,541→6,441, −1.5% w/w, with a Rp1.79tn foreign net-sell week), the cleanest idiosyncratic-strength signal this series has produced.
