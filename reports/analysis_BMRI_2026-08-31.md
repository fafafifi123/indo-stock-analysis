## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN

Seven days on from the Aug 24 report, BMRI's core thesis is unchanged and, on balance, incrementally strengthened rather than merely re-confirmed. No new fundamental data printed — Q3 2026 earnings remain the next hard catalyst, reconfirmed via search to still be due only in late October — so the valuation gap identified on Aug 12 and re-confirmed on Aug 24 (P/E ~5.9x, P/B ~1.0x, trailing dividend yield ~11.2%, all screening below/above their 5-year -2SD/+2SD bands against an 18.6% annualized ROE and 19.9% loan growth) persists entirely intact. What changed this window was flow and price, both modestly favorably: the price made a marginal new closing high (IDR 4,230 est. → IDR 4,250 confirmed Aug 28 close, +0.5%) after briefly dipping to ~4,140–4,170, and — more importantly — the foreign-selling fade that the Aug 24 report flagged tentatively (Aug 19's -IDR 40bn) has since gone further, flipping to outright net buying across Aug 21 (+140.0bn), Aug 26 (+205.3bn), and the Aug 24/26/28 cluster (+349.7bn cumulative), even as the month as a whole remains a net seller (-731bn) purely on the arithmetic weight of the mid-month MSCI spike. That is now a second consecutive window of evidence that the Aug 13 "dry run" gets absorbed and progressively reversed rather than compounding into a structural breakdown — precisely the pattern this thesis needs ahead of the binding November 2026 MSCI review. A minor, purely reputational item (a customer account freeze at law enforcement's request triggering a #BoikotBankMandiri social-media campaign) surfaced and was checked; it is financially immaterial and not a thesis input. These developments justify a modest score increase (54→55/70, Momentum 6→7) and a second consecutive stop tightening (3,950→4,000, reflecting twelve weeks of untested support), which together lift the base-case reward/risk to roughly 5.4:1 from 4.9:1, even as targets and the fundamental case itself are carried forward unchanged. This remains a *speculative*, not outright, Buy because November's MSCI outcome is still genuinely binary and macro-driven, GOTO's exclusion and the Rp50-floor slippage are reminders that Indonesia's index-accessibility story is still a work in progress, and two encouraging flow windows are not proof of how a full Frontier-track consultation would trade — but each week the spring low goes untested, and each MSCI-linked flow event gets absorbed and then unwound rather than compounding, adds incremental confidence that any November-driven flush would again be a buying opportunity rather than a fundamental impairment.
