> **The "Hidden Gem" Case:** BMTR is the quintessential Indonesian asset-play. Market cap of ~IDR 3.04T against total book equity of ~IDR 33.7T implies a P/B of **0.09x** — one of the most extreme holding-company discounts in Southeast Asia. Its subsidiaries (RCTI, MNCTV, GTV, iNews via MNCN; MNC Vision; MNC Life; MNC Land; MNC Pictures) have substantial independent value. The EBITDA margin of 29.54% shows the core media business still generates strong cash. **The discount exists for a reason** — structural TV advertising decline, governance concerns under Hary Tanoe, and holding-company premium. But at 0.09x P/B, even a partial catalyst could unlock 50–100% upside.

### ⚡ SPECULATIVE BUY — Asset Discount Play; Catalyst Required

BMTR represents a classic Indonesian "asset play" at an extreme holding-company discount (P/B 0.09x, EV/EBITDA 2.0x). The discount to intrinsic value is real and substantial. **The problem is that "cheap" can get cheaper** — without a specific catalyst (restructuring, MNCN privatisation, content monetisation deal), this discount can persist for years.

**Who should buy BMTR:** Investors with (1) a 12+ month horizon, (2) tolerance for holding-company governance risk, and (3) confidence in a specific near-term catalyst (2027 political advertising cycle, corporate restructuring).

**Who should not buy BMTR:** Capital appreciation seekers with a strict 6-month horizon and R/R minimum of 3:1. The 2.6:1 and lack of a clear near-term catalyst make this a position for patient value investors only.

**Execution:** Small position (3–5% portfolio weight) at IDR 186. Hard stop at IDR 145. Catalyst watch: MNCN price action, MNC Group restructuring news, political ad spend announcements.
