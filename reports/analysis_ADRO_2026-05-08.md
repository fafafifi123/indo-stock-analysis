## 1. The "Gem" Quality & Growth Filter

### 3-Year ROE & ROIC Trend — Inflection Analysis

| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 (Post-AADI) | Inflection? |
|--------|------|------|------|------------------|-------------|
| ROE | ~32% | ~22% | ~18% | ~17.8% | ⬇️ Normalizing (expected) |
| ROIC | ~28% | ~19% | ~12% | ~8.36% | ⬇️ CRITICAL: Near WACC |
| WACC | ~8% | ~8% | ~8.26% | ~8.26% | ⚠️ ROIC ≈ WACC |
| Net Revenue (USD B) | ~6.5B | ~6.5B | — | ~$1.5B* | ⬇️ Shrinking post-AADI |

*Post-AADI spinoff entity only (excludes thermal coal now in AADI)

**Critical Finding — ROIC vs WACC:** ADRO's ROIC of 8.36% vs WACC of 8.26% = economic profit spread of only 10bps. This is NOT the profile of a quality compounder. Every IDR of capital deployed earns almost exactly its cost — there is no compounding engine. This is the fundamental reason ADRO is classified as HOLD, not BUY.

**Inflection Potential:** If metcoal prices recover (hard coking coal demand from India/Southeast Asia steel sector), ROIC could re-expand to 12-15%. But this is a commodity price bet, not a business quality bet.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **Metallurgical Coal (Adaro Minerals / AMI):** Hard coking coal prices are more resilient than thermal coal. India's expanding steel sector requires HCC imports. ADRO has secured long-term offtake. Any positive pricing surprise → earnings beat.
2. **Indonesia Bauxite Export Ban (Alumina Value-Add):** Indonesia's ban on raw bauxite exports creates structural demand for domestic alumina processing. ADRO's INALUM alumina investment is positioned to benefit — but this is a 3-5 year story, not 6 months.
3. **High Dividend Yield (14.5%):** With 99.96% payout ratio, ADRO is essentially a high-yield income instrument. The dividend itself is a catalyst for income fund inflows.

### FCF / Cash Strength

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Dividend Yield | 14.55% | ✅ Exceptional for income |
| Payout Ratio | 99.96% | ⚠️ Unsustainable long-term (no reinvestment) |
| FCF (est. post-AADI) | ~USD 360M | ✅ Positive; strong cash generator |

**FCF Warning:** A 100% payout ratio is a double-edged sword. It signals: (1) management has no high-ROIC reinvestment opportunities internally; (2) they are distributing to shareholders rather than compounding. This is appropriate for an income play, but disqualifies it as a "gem" compounder.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

| Factor | Signal | Detail |
|--------|--------|--------|
| ROIC vs WACC | 🔴 CRITICAL | ROIC 8.36% ≈ WACC 8.26%; zero economic profit spread |
| Payout Ratio | ⚠️ 100% | No reinvestment = no compounding engine |
| Commodity Price Dependency | 🟡 CYCLICAL | Metcoal prices are market-driven; outside management control |
| Management Track Record | ✅ EXCELLENT | Thohir family + Adaro management = proven operators |
| Balance Sheet | ✅ CLEAN | Low leverage, strong cash post-AADI spinoff |

**Value Trap Risk Assessment: PARTIAL**

ADRO is not a classic value trap (assets are real, management is excellent, cash flows are positive). However, it COULD be a value trap if:
- New investments (alumina, renewables) generate ROIC < WACC
- Metcoal prices stay depressed for 2+ years
- The 100% payout means the company cannot reinvest for growth

**The Thohir Safety Net:** One reason ADRO avoids the value trap label is management quality. Garibaldi "Boy" Thohir has an outstanding track record of capital allocation. He spun off thermal coal at peak (created enormous value for shareholders via AADI) — this is the behavior of a capital allocator, not a value destroyer.
