### Value Trap Check

**Case against trap (strengthened further):** Net income still grew +21.9% YoY on margin discipline alone; the balance sheet is genuinely conservative (0.24x D/E, 3.74x current ratio); an independent, AAA-rated guarantor (CGIF/ADB) fully backstopped IDR 800B of new paper in June 2026 with no adverse rating action since; BI has now held rates flat across three consecutive meetings; and — new this cycle — BMTR has now demonstrably paid off a scheduled bond/sukuk tranche (Sept 14, Rp10.6bn) exactly on its contractual date, adding a second, independent, dated proof point of debt-service discipline alongside the 70-day silence on the much larger July 6 maturity. A company two months into a clean track record on its largest maturity, now also visibly current on a smaller unrelated one, is progressively weaker evidence for "trap" on the solvency dimension specifically — even as the float/concentration critique (now externally validated by FTSE, effective in one week) remains fully intact on the liquidity dimension.

**Verdict:** Unchanged in substance — not a classic value trap on solvency grounds, but a persistent, structural conglomerate discount and thin-float/concentration situation (now index-confirmed, with the mechanical removal itself just one week away) on real, cash-generative media/content assets. The near-term solvency question keeps getting more (not less) resolved by the passage of time and by actual on-schedule payments, while the float/liquidity constraint that slows any re-rating remains a live, near-term technical risk into Sept 21.
