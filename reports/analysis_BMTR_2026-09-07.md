## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check
**Case for trap (unchanged):** Revenue is shrinking (-4.6% YoY, FY2025) as legacy free-to-air TV advertising secularly declines; ROE (~4%) is mediocre in absolute terms; free float is thin (majority ownership sits with Hary Tanoesoedibjo/MNC Group entities, with Lo Kheng Hong holding a further ~6.4–6.5% / ~1.067B shares), which structurally caps re-rating velocity; conglomerate/holdco discounts on Indonesian media groups have persisted for years without closing. **New evidence this cycle actually reinforces this side of the ledger mechanically:** FTSE Russell's Sept 2026 removal of BMTR was explicitly a "High Shareholding Concentration" action — an independent, rules-based index provider confirming the exact thin-float/family-control structural feature this report has flagged since inception.

**Case against trap (strengthened further):** Net income still grew +21.9% YoY on margin discipline alone; the balance sheet is genuinely conservative (0.24x D/E, 3.74x current ratio); an independent, AAA-rated guarantor (CGIF/ADB) was willing to fully backstop IDR 800B of new paper in June 2026 and that guarantee rating has been reaffirmed since with no adverse action; and BI has now held rates flat across two consecutive meetings with no new hike in this window. A stock that a AAA guarantor underwrote at 7% and that has now traded through 63 event-free days is progressively weaker evidence for "trap" on the solvency dimension specifically, even as the float/concentration critique (now externally validated by FTSE) remains fully intact on the liquidity dimension.

**Verdict:** Unchanged in substance — not a classic value trap on solvency grounds, but a persistent, structural conglomerate discount and thin-float/concentration situation (now index-confirmed) on real, cash-generative media/content assets. The near-term solvency question keeps getting more (not less) resolved by the passage of time without incident, while the float/liquidity constraint that slows any re-rating has, if anything, just been reaffirmed by an independent third party.
