## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — MAINTAIN (conviction raised)

The wait is over, and the answer was a beat. BRI's H1/Q2 2026 results, released this morning, delivered exactly what three consecutive reports in this series had been positioned for: net profit of IDR 31.2 trillion, +17.5% YoY — an acceleration, not a deceleration, from Q1's own +13.8% — alongside loan growth of +16.2% YoY that leaves the FY2026 guidance band (7–9%) looking obsolete rather than merely conservative, and a continued downward trend in gross NPL to 3.15%. Two small items keep this from being an unambiguous green light: net NPL ticked up slightly to 1.04% even as gross NPL fell, and CASA slipped 50bps to 67.6% — neither thesis-breaking, both worth tracking into H2. The larger complication is timing: results landed before this morning's open, so the price this report is built on (IDR 3,190, Aug 28's close) is still a *pre-earnings* number, and we have no market reaction yet to weigh. Mechanically, R/R has improved to ~1.9:1 from 1.6:1 — the closest this series has come to our 2:1 sizing threshold — purely because the small pullback into the print left the stop and target unchanged against a slightly lower entry. We hold the Base (3,850) and Bull (4,500) targets unchanged, raise the Bear case to 3,100 now that the "H1 miss" scenario it was pricing is off the table, and leave the stop at 2,850. The next session's reaction to today's print, any sell-side target revisions in its wake, and whether the stock either breaks the 3,300 pivot on confirmed strength or offers a fill into the 3,000–3,100 add zone, are the specific triggers that would graduate this call to Strong Buy (Gem) next cycle. The November 2026 MSCI review remains the one item on the macro side still capable of overriding all of the above.
