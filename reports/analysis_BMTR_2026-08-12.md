## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check
**Case for trap:** Revenue is shrinking (-4.6% YoY, FY2025) as legacy free-to-air TV advertising secularly declines; ROE (~4%) is mediocre in absolute terms; free float is thin (majority ownership sits with Hary Tanoesoedibjo/MNC Group entities), which structurally caps re-rating velocity; conglomerate/holdco discounts on Indonesian media groups have persisted for years without closing.

**Case against trap:** Net income still grew +21.9% YoY on margin discipline alone; the balance sheet is genuinely conservative (0.24x D/E, 3.74x current ratio); an independent, AAA-rated guarantor (CGIF/ADB) was willing to fully backstop IDR 800B of new paper in June 2026 — that is a real third-party underwriting signal that professional credit investors did not view BMTR as distressed. A stock can be statistically cheap and still be a trap; a stock that a AAA guarantor just underwrote at 7% is a different, weaker case for "trap."

**Verdict:** Not a classic value trap — more accurately a persistent, structural conglomerate discount on real, cash-generative media/content assets, now with a resolved near-term solvency question.
