## Final Verdict: 🟡 Speculative Buy — DOWNGRADED from Buy

Three things changed since Sept 14. First, the price fell 33% in two weeks (115 → 77), most of it before and just after the Rp50 floor was removed, alongside MNC Group peers and with the company stating it knows of no material information. Second, two premises in the Sept 14 report did not hold up: BMTR does not appear on the FTSE Russell deletion or downgrade lists we could find, and the share count used (25.66B) conflicts with the ~16.4B implied by multiple sources; with MNCN trading at Rp136, the SOTP and the 225/285 targets built on them are withdrawn. Third, the H1 2026 financial statements remain unlocated for a fifth cycle, past the July 31 deadline, though no source we found names BMTR among the late filers or confirms a filing. On the positive side, there is no default, rating action, or adverse debt news; the Sept 14 small-tranche redemption is confirmed; the company said it provisioned Rp672.62bn for the July 6 maturity; and TTM P/E is ~2x. We therefore keep a positive but much more cautious stance: re-based targets of 115/160/50, a 66 stop (-14%), R/R 3.45:1, score 38/70, and sizing cut to 1–2% of a speculative allocation. Next steps: locate the H1 filing or any BEI notice, watch the 72–74 support and 86 resistance, and treat a reclaim of 86 as the first sign the group-wide selling has ended. Add toward full size only on an H1 confirmation.
