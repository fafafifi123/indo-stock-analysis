*FY2024 net loss largely driven by IDR 80.3T non-cash goodwill write-off from Tokopedia deconsolidation

**Quality Assessment:** GOTO cannot pass a traditional quality screen. There is NO 5-year track record of positive ROE or ROIC — the company has been burning cash since inception. The Q1 2026 profit is significant as a proof of concept but NOT yet a quality track record. An investor must underwrite the *future* not the *past*.

### Economic Moat

**Moat Type: Narrow but Improving**

After the Tokopedia deconsolidation, GOTO's remaining business has a cleaner moat story:

1. **Gojek — Transport/On-Demand:** ~50M+ monthly active users. Indonesia's dominant super-app for mobility (GoRide, GoCar), food delivery (GoFood), and merchant services. Network effects are real: more drivers = better service = more users.
2. **GoPay — Fintech:** 2025 marked GoPay's first full year of profitability (EBITDA IDR 497B). GoPay is embedded in Gojek + merchant networks. Growing into lending, insurance distribution, investment products.
3. **GoTo Logistics:** Beneficiary of Indonesia's e-commerce boom (even as Tokopedia deconsolidated, logistics demand from TikTok-Tokopedia continues).

**Growth Catalysts:**
- Indonesia's digital economy: projected to reach $130B by 2025, $300B+ by 2030
- GoPay monetization accelerating (PayLater, loans)
- Institutional trust building: Alibaba lock-up + $200M share buyback signals confidence
- B2B services (GoSend, Midtrans payments) growing faster than consumer
- AI integration in routing, fraud detection, and personalization improving margins
