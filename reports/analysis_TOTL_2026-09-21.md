Rather than mechanically concede the tier, this cycle resolves the trigger by **trailing the stop from IDR 1,120 to IDR 1,300** — the first stop adjustment in this series. The justification: the Sept 14 report explicitly withheld a stop trail pending "a second cycle of confirmation" of the pivot holding; that confirmation arrived this week (a firmer hold than the first, with the week's low at 1,480 vs. the pivot at 1,475, plus a genuine — if unconfirmed — resistance pierce to 1,615). IDR 1,300 sits just below the old 1,300–1,350 add zone, still well clear of the 1,475 pivot, so it is not a knife's-edge stop vulnerable to normal noise, but it does lock in a materially smaller loss than the old 1,120 level. On this trailed stop, R/R recovers to **1.71:1**.

**This is a risk-management fix, not a reward-side improvement** — the target remains capped at IDR 1,950 pending the Oct 29 print, and the upside percentage keeps shrinking every cycle regardless of what happens to the stop. **This remains a Hold-existing/no-new-entries stance.** Full-size new entries should still wait for either (a) a pullback toward the IDR 1,300–1,350 zone — now further away than ever — which would restore R/R to roughly 2:1+, or (b) an October 29 Q3 beat that lifts the DCF target and restores headroom from the top. The real story this cycle, though, is mechanical: with the DCF target still fixed at IDR 1,950 pending the Oct 29 Q3 print and the stop still fixed at the Aug-era IDR 1,120, the further 2.7% price gain to IDR 1,540 pushed the static Risk/Reward to **0.98:1** — the exact parity-breach the Sept 14 report flagged as a trigger to revisit the tier. Rather than downgrade on that mechanical trigger alone, this report trails the stop to IDR 1,300 — justified by the second consecutive week of pivot confirmation the prior report said it was waiting for — which restores R/R to a workable 1.71:1, though this is explicitly a risk-management fix on the downside, not a reward-side improvement; the upside percentage (now +26.6%, down from +30.0% last week, and the fifth straight cycle of this pattern) keeps shrinking regardless. Netting a Momentum upgrade (firmer pivot hold, first genuine resistance pierce) against further Value compression and a genuinely worse R/R picture even after the stop trail, the score ticks down one more point to **51/70**, still inside the Speculative Buy band (35–54) but continuing to drift toward its middle. This remains a **Hold-existing, no-new-entries** name: the newly trailed IDR 1,300 stop protects a larger share of gains for existing holders than the old IDR 1,120 level, but new capital should continue to wait for either a pullback toward the IDR 1,300–1,350 zone or an October 29 earnings beat strong enough to lift the target and restore headroom from the top. If a third pivot-hold week fails to materialize, or if Q3 disappoints, the next report should treat the tier itself — not just the stop — as under review.
