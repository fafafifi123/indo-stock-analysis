#### MSCI Exclusion — Effective Today (Aug 31, 2026)

#### IDX Rp50 Floor Removal (Targeted September 7, 2026) — Now One Week Closer
Unchanged in timeline (still targeted Sept 7; second member test ran Aug 29) but meaningfully worse in the underlying evidence: the sell queue has grown to 320–419M lots (from 246M on Aug 24), and the negotiated market — the best available proxy for a post-removal clearing price — has averaged just ~Rp22/share across a 26.5M-lot sample since Aug 3. The offsetting consideration, also new this cycle: Morgan Stanley's Aug 21 stake increase to 5.15% at Rp23/share is a real institutional buyer transacting size at levels close to that nego-market average, suggesting at least one large holder views current implied pricing as attractive rather than as a falling knife. This remains the single most important open question for the next report in this series.

#### NEW — 8% Commission Cap: Confirmed, Quantified, Already-Live Structural Drag
This is the risk factor the task brief specifically flagged as under-weighted in the Aug 24 report, and verification confirms it deserves a dedicated stress test. The cap took effect **July 1, 2026** under Kepmenhub Decree No. 532/2026 (implementing Presidential Regulation No. 27/2026, following President Prabowo's May Day 2026 call for lower platform commissions), cutting GoTo's maximum take-rate on two-wheeler rides (GoRide) from roughly 20% to 8% and forcing the discontinuation of the "GoRide Hemat" subscription-discount driver programme. Management now guides to an **~Rp300 billion hit to FY26 Adjusted EBITDA** from the cap — the first hard number attached to a risk the Aug 24 report treated only qualitatively ("already reflected in guidance"). The mitigating facts: (a) the cap applies specifically to two-wheeler rides, a segment that is ~7% of Group net revenue; (b) On-Demand segment adjusted EBITDA still grew (Q2: +41% YoY to ~Rp464B) despite absorbing the cap; (c) Group guidance of Rp3.2–3.4T Adjusted EBITDA was held, not cut, because fintech's guidance raise offset the on-demand trim. The residual risk is policy-path risk: this cap was imposed via direct presidential pressure in a single legislative cycle, and there is no evidence found either confirming or ruling out further tightening (e.g., extension to four-wheel GoCar services, or a lower cap still). This is a real, live, and now-quantified earnings headwind — not a resolved one — and should be weighted as such in any position-sizing decision alongside the MSCI and floor-removal risks.

## Final Verdict: ⚡ Speculative Buy — HELD, not further downgraded

Two weeks that were supposed to bring resolution instead brought confirmation on both sides of the thesis, in roughly offsetting measure. The mechanical overhang genuinely worsened: the sell queue at the Rp50 floor grew from ~246 million to 320–419 million lots, negotiated-market trading — the best available proxy for GOTO's true off-floor value — has averaged only ~Rp22/share since early August, and the MSCI exclusion that was merely "pending an effective date" on Aug 24 is executing as of today's close. The 8% commission cap, treated qualitatively in the prior report, is now confirmed as a live, quantified ~Rp300 billion FY26 EBITDA drag under formal government regulation, not a mere proposal. Set against all of that, the fundamental case got hard, disclosed numbers behind it for the first time this cycle: a confirmed Rp607.5 billion H1 net profit swing, +75% YoY GTV growth, Group Adjusted EBITDA above Rp1 trillion for the first time, and — most tellingly — a real institutional buyer in Morgan Stanley increasing its stake to just above 5% via a Rp87.3 billion negotiated-market purchase ten days before the MSCI effective date, at a price close to where the broader nego market has been clearing. On balance, this report **holds** the Speculative Buy rating rather than downgrading further: the ~IDR 17.3/share cash anchor still sits *above* even the market's own de facto (nego-market) price discovery, the buyback remains active, and the business itself is unambiguously improving even as the technical mechanics remain the dominant near-term driver of the share price. The score falls modestly, from 48 to 46, to reflect the queue growth and the newly quantified commission-cap risk, and the Bear case is lowered from IDR 35 to IDR 32 to reflect real (not assumed) evidence of where the stock might clear once the Rp50 floor is lifted. Existing holders should keep the IDR 38 reference stop unchanged; new capital should treat the September 7 floor-removal date, still one week away, as the decisive near-term catalyst — and should not read Morgan Stanley's stake increase as a signal that the floor-removal gap-down risk has been de-risked, only that it has at least one large buyer on the other side of it.
