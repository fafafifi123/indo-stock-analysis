**ROE Assessment:** BMRI's ROE has maintained above 20% for three consecutive years — the gold standard for Indonesian banking. Unlike BBRI, BMRI's ROE has NOT deteriorated, reflecting its more diversified loan book (corporate/wholesale-heavy, less micro exposure). The 2025 ROE guidance is "above 20%".

**ROIC / Return on Assets:** Estimated ROA of ~3.0-3.3%, indicating strong asset deployment efficiency. BMRI's loan portfolio grows at 16-17% (excl. BRIS deconsolidation) while keeping NPL in check.

### Economic Moat

**Moat Type: Wide Moat**

BMRI is Indonesia's largest bank by assets and a dominant player in the corporate banking, trade finance, and transactional banking segments. Moat sources:

1. **Corporate banking leadership:** BMRI finances ~25% of all syndicated loans in Indonesia; irreplaceable relationships with state-owned enterprises and large conglomerates
2. **Livin' super-app:** 38M+ users; digital transactions growing 20%+ annually; fee income from Livin' up 19.8% YoY — building a sticky ecosystem
3. **Subsidiary diversification:** Mandiri Tunas Finance (auto), AXA Mandiri (insurance), Mandiri Sekuritas (brokerage) — cross-sell engine diversifying revenue
4. **Government float deposits:** Receives significant low-cost government funds
5. **Scale:** IDR 2.1 quadrillion in assets — impossible for new entrants to replicate

**Growth Catalysts:**
- Corporate loan expansion (infrastructure, energy transition, data centers)
- Livin' ecosystem monetization (payments, investments, insurance distribution)
- BRIS (Bank Syariah Indonesia) — BMRI deconsolidated BRIS in 2025, cleaning financials; BMRI still holds 51% stake
- Indonesia's rising middle class increasing retail banking penetration
- Rate cut cycle (BI) expected to expand NIM in H2 2026

## 2. Governance & Risk

### Corporate Governance (GCG)
- **Board:** Experienced management team under President Director Darmawan Junaidi; independent commissioner board
- **Transparency:** Quarterly earnings call, robust IR disclosure
- **ESG commitment:** Mandiri launched green financing framework; significant exposure to renewable project finance
- **Related-party:** Standard SOE operational ties; no significant controversies
- **GCG Red Flag Score: LOW** — Best-in-class governance among Indonesian state banks

### Value Trap Assessment

**Is BMRI a value trap? NO**

BMRI trades at P/E 7-9x versus Indonesian banking peer average of 17-21x. The discount is explained by:
1. SOE discount (government interference risk)
2. Post-BRIS deconsolidation accounting adjustments in 2025
3. Market rotation out of banking in 2025

This is NOT structural decline. BMRI's loan growth (16-17% excl. BRIS), fee income growth (12.8%), and stable NPL confirm fundamental strength.

**Key Risk to Monitor:** NIM compression — NIM fell to 4.8% in Q1 2025 (from 5.3% in Q4 2024) due to higher funding costs and deposit repricing. If BI holds rates longer than expected, NIM may remain compressed. However, CoC (cost of credit) improved to 0.88% (very healthy).
