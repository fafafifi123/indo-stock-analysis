## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

GOTO does **not** screen as a classic value trap despite three-plus months at the floor: (a) fundamentals are inflecting positive — two consecutive profitable quarters and a first-ever >IDR 1T EBITDA quarter; (b) the balance sheet carries ~35% of market cap in net cash with essentially no debt, providing a hard margin of safety; (c) the price stagnation is a **market-structure artifact** — the IDX Rp50 minimum tick floor plus forced passive selling (FTSE Russell exit completed in June, MSCI freeze since May) rather than deteriorating fundamentals; (d) the buyback is actively absorbing float. The real risk is **mechanical, not fundamental**: illiquidity could persist even as earnings improve if index-fund forced selling keeps dominating flow — a technical trap layered on top of an improving business, which is precisely the special-situations setup this framework is built to exploit.

### Stress Tests

#### IDR Weakens to 19,500
A ~9% depreciation from current ~17,870. GOTO's revenue is overwhelmingly domestic-GTV-denominated with limited direct FX exposure, but the real USD value of the IDR 23.7T cash pile erodes, and cloud/tech import costs tick up modestly. More material: an EM risk-off move would likely halt or reverse the IHSG's +8.6% rally, delaying float normalization and dampening any MSCI-relief-rally scenario. Moderate negative, not thesis-breaking.

#### BI Rate +50bps
A hike to 6.25% (from 5.75%) to defend the rupiah has negligible direct effect given GOTO's near-zero interest-bearing debt, but raises the cost of capital broadly — DCF discount rate ticks up ~50–100bps, compressing base-case intrinsic value by roughly IDR 4–6/share — and could dampen small/micro-cap risk appetite on the IHSG, delaying liquidity normalization for GOTO specifically.

#### MSCI Frontier/Exclusion Downgrade — November 2026 Review
Tonight's (Aug 12) decision addresses the immediate index-status question; this scenario contemplates a further confirmation/hardening of exclusion at the November semi-annual review. Impact: additional forced selling from remaining MSCI Indonesia trackers (~4% GOTO weight as of end-July), but as Aletheia Capital's Angus Mackintosh notes, the incremental impact is likely "minimal" since the stock is already effectively suspended/untradeable at the floor — most passive flow has already been mechanically blocked or priced out. The larger risk is reputational — reinforcing the "national champion that fell from grace" narrative — rather than fresh mechanical selling pressure, since liquidity (not index membership) is already the binding constraint.

## Final Verdict: 💎 Strong Buy (Gem) — MAINTAIN

GoTo's fundamental turnaround is no longer a single-quarter fluke: a second consecutive profitable quarter, adjusted EBITDA crossing IDR 1 trillion for the first time, fintech profitability now exceeding on-demand, and FY26 group EBITDA guidance held firm despite a real regulatory headwind all argue the business itself is inflecting durably. Against that, the stock remains mechanically imprisoned at the IDX's IDR 50 floor for a 14th straight week, has been demoted a listing board, lost its FTSE Russell membership, and faces a same-day binary MSCI verdict that could swing sentiment sharply in either direction before this report is even a day old. That combination — improving fundamentals colliding with intensifying technical/liquidity risk — is exactly the special-situations profile this framework targets, and the 5.6:1 risk/reward with a 35%-of-market-cap net-cash cushion still justifies a Strong Buy stance, though the 3-point score reduction versus July 4 (57→54) reflects the genuine, near-term two-sided volatility the MSCI decision introduces. Existing holders should maintain positions through the IDR 45 stop (an FCA-placement trigger, not a normal technical stop); new capital should size for the possibility that the floor persists for several more weeks even as fundamentals continue to improve.
