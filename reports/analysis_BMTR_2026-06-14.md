## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend

| Year | ROE | ROIC | Revenue (IDR T) | EBITDA (IDR T) | EBITDA Margin |
|------|-----|------|-----------------|----------------|---------------|
| 2023 | 3.2% | 3.8% | 10.1 | 2.9 | 28.7% |
| 2024 | 2.8% | 3.2% | 9.9 | 2.8 | 28.3% |
| 2025 (TTM) | 2.4% | 2.9% | **9.8** | **2.9** | **29.5%** |

**Inflection Analysis:** BMTR shows no ROE/ROIC improvement — these are structurally LOW at 2-3%, far below cost of equity (~12%). The business earns less than its cost of capital every year. This is the fundamental contradiction: the EBITDA margin is excellent (29.5%) but depreciation, amortization, and interest costs mean net earnings are thin, yielding poor ROE.

**Key Insight:** BMTR's P&L is NOT the reason to own it. The thesis is pure **asset play** — you're buying IDR 33.7T in equity (MNCN stations, MNC Vision, MNC studios, land) for IDR 2.1T.

### Revenue Catalysts — Assessment

**Structural Headwinds (Real):**
- FTA television advertising revenue declining globally; digital advertising taking share
- RCTI/MNCTV/GTV/iNews audience share declining vs. YouTube, TikTok, Netflix
- Pay TV subscriber base declining (cord-cutting to streaming)

**Potential Catalysts (Speculative):**
- **Digital Pivot:** MNC Media assets could be monetized on OTT/streaming platform
- **Asset Monetization:** Hary Tanoe group could sell MNCN to strategic buyer (MNC is often rumored in M&A speculation)
- **Undervalued Land Bank:** MNC group owns real estate in Jakarta (Kebon Jeruk MNC Center) — unvalued on books

**Revenue Trend:** Flat at ~IDR 9.8-10.1T for 3 consecutive years. No growth catalyst for the core FTA business.

### Cash Strength & FCF

- **D/E Ratio:** 21.6% — LOW leverage; strong balance sheet resilience
- **Interest Coverage:** Estimated >8x — BI rate hike has MINIMAL P&L impact
- **FCF:** Positive; EBITDA ~IDR 2.9T; capex ~IDR 400-600M = strong FCF at operating level
- **Distribution:** Sporadic dividends; not consistent income vehicle like banks
