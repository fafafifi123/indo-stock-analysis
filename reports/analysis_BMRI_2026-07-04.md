## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value Trap Check

Profit is flat, not declining; the NIM squeeze is BI-rate-cyclical, not franchise erosion. Corporate NPLs benign. State-owned overhang (dividend extraction, policy lending) is real but constant — it does not explain a 5-year-low multiple by itself. **NOT a value trap; a rate-cycle hostage with a 9%+ yield while you wait.**

### Hidden Assets

Mandiri Sekuritas, Mandiri Capital (VC), AXA Mandiri insurance JV, Bank Syariah Indonesia (BSI) stake — the BSI stake alone is a top-tier Islamic banking franchise carried without market-value recognition.
