## 1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter

**Efficiency:** 9M 2026 profit Rp45.5tn (+20.7%), but Q3 alone was only +14%, so growth is slowing. Opex -9% and a CIR guidance cut to 37-39% are real gains. NIM went 4.4% → ~4.2% (Q3) → ~4.0% (Oct, bank-only), and loans-at-risk rose to 6.8% from 5.8%. **Inflection: no.** Costs are improving while margin and asset quality soften, and the 25bp hike adds pressure on funding costs.

**Growth drivers (next 2 quarters):** corporate loans +22%, the GWM cut, fee income +13.8%. Corporate lending reprices faster than BBRI's micro book, a partial offset to the hike.

**Cash strength:** dividend-driven; no dilution.
