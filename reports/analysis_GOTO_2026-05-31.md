**THE INFLECTION OF INFLECTIONS — Q1 2026:**

GOTO reported its **first-ever net profit (IDR 171B)** in Q1 2026, reversing a net loss of IDR 367B in Q1 2025. This is not just a financial milestone — it is a **psychological re-rating event** for Indonesia's most-watched tech stock. The key metrics driving the pivot:

- **Adj. EBITDA: +131% YoY** to IDR 907B — profitability is not a fluke but driven by operating leverage
- **Adj. FCF: +IDR 1.3T** — the company is generating real cash, not accounting earnings
- **Revenue: +26% YoY** — growth is accelerating, not slowing, as it turns profitable
- **GTV: +63% YoY** to IDR 236T — e-commerce and on-demand at scale

This is the moment GOTO transitions from "loss-making tech promise" to "emerging profitable digital ecosystem." The market has not fully re-priced this reality.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters

1. **E-commerce (Tokopedia TikTok Shop integration):** Post-TikTok Shop merger, Tokopedia now carries significant e-commerce GMV. The full-year effect of this integration will drive GTV growth.
2. **Ride-hailing recovery:** GoRide/GoCar benefiting from Indonesia's urban mobility growth; 69M users × increasing frequency.
3. **GoTo Financial:** GoPay/GoPayLater (fintech) growing rapidly — embedded finance within the ecosystem drives high-margin lending and payment revenue.
4. **Operating leverage:** With revenue growing faster than costs, each IDR of incremental revenue flows disproportionately to profit. EBITDA margin expanding quarter-by-quarter.

### Cash Strength & FCF

- **Adj. FCF Q1 2026: IDR +1.3T** — strongest-ever quarterly free cash flow
- Cash runway: GOTO holds significant cash from IPO proceeds; no near-term capital raise needed
- **No dividend** (growth company reinvesting for scale)

- **FCF per share annualised: IDR ~4.8** vs current price IDR 53 → FCF yield ~9% (if sustainable)
