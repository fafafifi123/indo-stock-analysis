Hold the position with the existing 580 stop, treat 590–625 as the (revised, lower) accumulation zone rather than adding aggressively, and treat the Aug 19–20 BI decision and any Q3 operational commentary as the next real tests of the thesis. A breach of 580 should be treated as thesis invalidation, not a buying opportunity.
