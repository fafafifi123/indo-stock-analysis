## Final Verdict: Hold — MAINTAIN (Waiting for Flow and Q3 Confirmation)

BBRI traded at IDR 3,030 on Oct 8 (-1.3% since the Oct 2 print), after a ~3.5% drop on the day IHSG fell 1.88% to 6,031.28, and was ~3,050 on Oct 9 morning; the Oct 9 close was not found. Valuation is deep (~7.4x TTM P/E, ~11.4% yield, ~1.2x P/B), consensus targets cluster near our IDR 3,860 base, and credit metrics are acceptable, so this is not a Value Trap or Avoid. But the conditions to restore Speculative Buy are not met: BBRI sits just above the 3,000 support after failing to hold 3,100; foreign selling is a repeated theme with Oct 5-9 amounts unknown (the "Rp3.12tn net sell as IHSG surges" headline dates from June 10 and was excluded); macro is hostile (hawkish FOMC minutes, Fed 3.75-4.00%, IDR near 18,000, market YTD foreign net sell Rp82.55tn); and no new fundamentals have been published since the August monthly data. The 6.4:1 paper R/R (3.6:1 vs bear) rests on a stop 4.3% away that sits within gap-risk zones. Upgrade triggers: a close above ~3,200, two consecutive weeks of neutral-to-positive BBRI foreign flow, and a Q3 print (expected around end-October, unconfirmed) showing CASA/NIM stabilising. Downgrade triggers: a sustained break below 3,000/2,900, IDR above 18,000, or an adverse MSCI signal.
