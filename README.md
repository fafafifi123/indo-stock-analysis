## Summary Findings

### Top % Quality Compounders (Tier 1)
- **BMRI** — Best-in-class Indonesian bank. ROE >21%, improving asset quality, cheapest valuation among big banks (P/E ~7-9x). Strong loan growth momentum. Accumulation signals from institutions.
- **TOTL** — Hidden gem in construction. Debt-free, record profits, data center theme driving backlog. P/E ~9-10x with 25%+ earnings growth. Under-covered by retail.

### Recovery / Income Plays (Tier 2)
- **BBRI** — Structurally sound but cyclically stressed by micro-NPL blowup. Long-term franchise intact, but need 2-3 quarters of NPL stabilization before re-rating. 9.4% dividend yield offers cushion.
- **ADRO** — Post-coal-spinoff transformation. Extremely cheap (P/E ~3-7x), 14%+ dividend yield. Not a quality compounder — a pure income/deep-value trade with transformation optionality.

### Deep Value / Small-Cap Watch
- **GPRA** — P/E 4.3x with 45%+ sales growth. Severely mispriced if growth sustains. Liquidity risk and property cycle risk are key caveats.

## Framework Used
1. **Quality Filter:** ROE/ROIC 5-year trend, moat assessment, FCF generation
2. **Governance & Risk:** GCG red flags, value trap check, structural decline test
3. **Valuation:** P/E, P/B, PEG, conservative DCF with margin of safety
4. **Momentum:** Institutional flow, price trend, accumulation/distribution signals
5. **Stress Test:** Bear case assumptions on growth, NPL, commodity cycles
