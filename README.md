## Key Findings Summary

### ⭐ Tier 1 — Hidden Gems (Strong Buy)

**BMRI — Bank Mandiri** *(Updated May 31, 2026)*
- ROE 22.1% — best-in-class, 5-year high; Q1 2026 profit +16.6% YoY to IDR 15.4T
- P/E 7.0x vs. 5-year mean 12.5x; near P/E trough despite accelerating fundamentals
- BRIS deconsolidation removes accounting noise → cleaner re-rating story
- 10.6% dividend yield = "paid to wait" protection; asset-sensitive balance sheet = rate cut tailwind
- **Hidden asset:** IDR 24T excess capital (19.7% CAR vs 12% minimum); Mandiri Sekuritas, AXA Mandiri subsidiaries
- DCF range: IDR 5,400–7,800; 6M target IDR 5,800 (+42%)

**TOTL — Total Bangun Persada** *(Updated May 31, 2026)*
- Profits TRIPLED in 3 years (IDR 91B → IDR 265B FY2024; IDR 390B+ FY2025E)
- ROIC 24-28% with ZERO DEBT — extraordinary combination
- STT GDC (Singapore data center) became #1 customer with 30x revenue growth YoY
- Data center construction supercycle = 5-10 year tailwind; multiple hyperscalers building Indonesia DCs
- Market still prices TOTL as commodity constructor at 7.5x forward P/E — major re-rating potential
- FCF yield 18.7%; debt-free = most resilient to rate hikes in this portfolio

### ⚠️ Tier 2 — Speculative Buys (Recovery / Discovery)

**BBRI — Bank Rakyat Indonesia** *(Updated May 31, 2026)*
- **INFLECTION CONFIRMED Q1 2026:** Net profit +13.8% YoY (IDR 15.63T); beat expectations
- Micro NPL formation rate falling from 6.57% → 4.82% — the crisis is peaking, not deepening
- BRImo digital banking: 47.8M users (+18.6%), transactions IDR 2,042T (+29.4%)
- 9.1% dividend yield provides powerful downside protection
- Catalyst: Q2 2026 micro NPL ratio (target <3.8%) + BI rate cut

**GPRA — Perdana Gapura Prima** *(Updated May 31, 2026)*
- P/E 4.3x; P/B 0.51x (trading below net asset value); analyst target IDR 170-185
- H1 2024 presales +45% YoY → backlog converting to revenue H2 2025-H1 2026
- Early discovery signal: +23.23% in one month, +9.91% single-day spike
- Affordable housing policy + BI rate cuts = structural demand tailwind
- Risk: Small-cap illiquidity; max position 8%

**GOTO — GoTo Gojek Tokopedia** *(Updated May 31, 2026)*
- **HISTORIC MILESTONE:** First-ever quarterly net profit IDR 171B (Q1 2026)
- Revenue +26% YoY; GTV +63% to IDR 236T; 69M transacting users (+22%)
- Adj. EBITDA +131% YoY; Adj. FCF positive IDR 1.3T — real cash generation
- New buyer base: Institutional funds previously excluded from loss-making GOTO now eligible
- Risk: P/E 122x; 1.083T shares; EV/EBITDA 43x — expensive; binary risk

**TBLA — Tunas Baru Lampung** *(Upgraded from Avoid; Updated May 31, 2026)*
- B50 mandate starts July 1, 2026 → increases domestic FAME demand ~40% above B35
- 722,000 KL biodiesel delivery contracts already locked for FY2026
- Q1 2026 net profit IDR 275B — strongest quarter in 3 years; revenue +8.7% YoY
- Analyst Buy (Bareksa, May 12): target IDR 960 (+33%)
- Risk: D/E 193%; interest coverage 2.1x; governance 8/54 — STRICT STOP LOSS REQUIRED

**BMTR — Global Mediacom** *(NEW — First Coverage May 31, 2026)*
- RCTI, MNCTV, GTV, iNews = Indonesia's largest FTA TV portfolio; 76%+ Indonesians watch FTA TV
- FY2025: Revenue IDR 9.59T; EBITDA IDR 3.04T (margin 29.5%); net income surged +106% to IDR 1.11T
- **HIDDEN ASSET PLAY:** Sum-of-parts value IDR 276-440/share vs price IDR 141 = 50-68% holdco discount
- EV/EBITDA 4.6x; FCF yield 21.8% — extraordinarily cheap if revenue stabilizes
- Risk: -22% price decline in 5 weeks (technical weakness); secular TV ad headwind; illiquid

### 💰 Tier 3 — Hold / Income Play

**ADRO — Alamtri Resources** *(Updated May 31, 2026)*
- 10.6% dividend yield; USD revenue = IDR weakness hedge; net cash balance sheet
- ROIC 8.36% ≈ WACC 8.26% → near-zero economic profit; not a GARP compounder
- Alumina project (51.2% of profit by 2028E) is the transformation catalyst — but far away
- 100% dividend payout = zero reinvestment; alumina capex funding path unclear
- **For income portfolios: HOLD. For growth portfolios: UNDERWEIGHT.**

## Methodology Notes

**Framework Applied:** GARP (Growth at Reasonable Price) + Special Situations
**Sections per stock:**
1. Gem Quality & Growth Filter — ROE/ROIC 3-year trend, inflection analysis, revenue catalysts, FCF
2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment — Value trap check, hidden assets, asset turnover
3. Valuation & Sentiment — P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA vs 5-year bands; 2-stage DCF; 6-month target
4. Bandarmology & Momentum — Volume patterns, foreign flow, accumulation/distribution, pivot point
5. Execution & Stress Test — R/R ratio; IDR/USD shock; BI rate shock scenarios
6. Final Verdict — Scorecard + Buy/Hold/Sell + position sizing guidance
