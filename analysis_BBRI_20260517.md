## 1. The Gem Quality & Growth Filter

### ROE & ROIC — 3-Year Trend

| Year | ROE | NIM | NPL Micro | CoC | Comment |
|------|-----|-----|-----------|-----|---------|
| 2023 | 21.2% | 8.4% | 3.1% | 2.5% | Peak cycle — micro boom |
| 2024 | 18.6% | 7.9% | 6.57% | 3.8% | NPL surge — sentiment crash |
| 2025 | 17.3% | 7.8% | 4.82% | 3.2% | **Inflection — NPL normalizing** |
| 2026E | 18.0% | 7.6% | 3.9–4.0% | 2.9–3.0% | Recovery year |

**Inflection Signal:** ROE bottomed and is stabilizing. NPL peaked in H1 2025 and is declining sequentially each quarter — this is the "max pessimism" setup in a structurally sound franchise. Every 50bps improvement in micro NPL unlocks ~IDR 8–10T of provisioning releases, flowing directly to net income. The earnings re-rating has only begun.

### Revenue Catalysts — Next 2 Quarters (Q2–Q3 2026)

1. **NPL Write-Back Cycle:** As micro NPL normalizes toward guided 3.9–4.0%, cost of credit drops from 3.2% toward 2.9%, releasing provisions. This alone can add IDR 3–5T in H2 2026 net income — not yet priced into consensus.
2. **Agricultural Lending Season Peak:** Q2–Q3 is the peak agricultural credit cycle in Indonesia. BRI's 10,000+ Unit network across Java, Sumatra, and Sulawesi is uniquely positioned to absorb this demand. 7–9% loan growth guidance is likely conservative.
3. **BRIAPI & Digital Fee Income:** BRI's open banking platform processes >2B transactions/month. Digital fee income growing 15%+ YoY — diversifying revenue away from NIM sensitivity.
4. **Government KUR Expansion:** BRI is the primary channel for Kredit Usaha Rakyat (government-subsidized micro lending). Budget expansion for 2026 directly enlarges BRI's addressable micro loan market.
